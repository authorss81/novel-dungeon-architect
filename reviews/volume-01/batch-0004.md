The findings in `logs/batch-0004.review.log` were treated as authoritative. Every blocking finding and every state-file defect has been applied. Findings 22 and 23 were watch items and are recorded below as such.

## Blocking repairs applied

- **B1 — Chapter 40 certified a term it broke.** Clause 2 of the Chapter 38 terms requires the party out of the step "in under a minute"; Chapter 40 said "in ninety seconds … which is the one term on the page that has been satisfied every time." Ninety seconds is not under a minute, and Chapters 33 and 37 ran fifty-one and fifty seconds. The figure is now **forty-nine seconds**, and the prose notes that it was faster than either of the two times before it, so the term is the chapter's earned one rather than a summary.
- **B2 — duplicated clause and wrong attribution in Chapter 39.** The first step read "It is opened and traced, **It is opened and traced,** and the void…" with a stranded capital, and it was attributed to Iven on a page that is Marek's plan and is corrected four lines later in his own words. The duplicate is gone and the line now reads "said Marek."
- **B3 — broken sentence in Chapter 40's central paragraph.** "They came in past the cord and looked for a long time. **The lit part of the work is over:**" left a clause in front of a colon that was not a sentence. The paragraph now closes its first sentence properly.
- **B3a — the lamp was never reconciled with Renn's written refusal.** Chapter 38 has Renn's own term, read back before nine people: "no lamp, no stand, no bench on my landing." Chapter 40 carried a lamp stand down four times and never once mentioned the term. This is now a staged scene, and it is the chapter's second-best beat: Jo reads the line aloud, points out that the stand is on the step *below* Renn's landing and past the cord, Renn says she can hear its legs being packed from the room she sleeps in, and then Renn dictates her own reconciliation onto the slate and makes Jo put her name under it, because a line in a boundary's hand that somebody else wrote out is read in a year as the boundary not knowing. The refusal is honoured and the work is still possible.
- **B4 — Chapter 37's number contradicted Chapter 37.** Sefu said "**Forty** minutes and fifty seconds is not a stop" against four minutes and six seconds going to four minutes and fifty in the same chapter, with nineteen minutes of room closure. Now **four** minutes and fifty, and the two lengths he contrasts — fifty seconds, nineteen minutes — match the figure as written.
- **B5 — Chapter 35's wedge arithmetic broke twice.** "**Six of the seven are spoken for**" was followed by an enumeration of three waist + three head + one basket, which is seven, and the chapter closed on "**eight** wedges went into three places and a basket" against a canon of seven sound. Canon is now the Batch 0005 prompt's own figure: **seven sound wedges and two dead sticks — three at the head, three at the waist, one in the basket** — and Tams's opening line distinguishes the six in use from the seventh in the basket, which is the reserve. Marek's existing line at 35:17 ("six spoken for … leaves one in the basket") and the Chapter 35 allocation at 35:95 and 35:99 were already correct and are now supported by the enumeration above them.
- **B6 — two different "fifth nulls."** Chapter 31 listed four prior nulls and then wrote one, making itself the fifth; Chapter 33 said "four times … and he wrote it a fifth time," which would have made Chapter 31 the sixth. Chapter 33 is the fifth in Chapter 37, Chapter 40, and all five state files, so the fix is one word of Chapter 31: the twenty-third day comes out of the list. Chapter 31 is now the fourth, Chapter 33 the fifth, and every downstream figure is unchanged. The state files needed no edit, which is the reason this was the right way round.
- **B7 — the eight-foot draw cord could not have two feet still out.** Nera's cord is eight feet and "went down about eight feet," so it was fully paid out when the belay came up. It now comes up with **a foot** still out. Related: Iven's "this building has **eighteen feet of line** in it and every foot of it is spoken for" sat six lines from his own Chapter 36 tally of a forty-foot black cord nobody releases. He now says there are eight feet of line in the building that come free, and adds that he asked Sefu about the black cord in the second week and was told no and has not asked again — which puts the boundary's refusal on the page. The eighteen-foot hand line is now sourced in prose, in Tams's mouth in Chapter 37, so the continuity file's figure has an anchor. The rhetorical range becomes "eight feet and forty feet and any length at all."
- **B8 — three watch posts stood empty, unremarked, and one was load-bearing.** In Chapters 33 and 37 the wheel, the threshold, and the landing are explicitly manned. In Chapter 40 the wheel, the threshold, and the landing all go down the stairs with the party, and Sefu's own new answer relies on Dalla being "at a threshold and not on a stair" while she is writing on a landing. Chapter 40 now states the rule the whole chapter needs — **a post is only a post while its condition is happening**, so with the corridor shut the waist was not a post at all — and names every substitute: Iven on the wheel, the woman from the wash rooms on Renn's landing bracket after Mikka asks her, Mikka on the clinic return and the frame, and **Ruk Sella on the threshold bell**, who said yes in one word and was not told by anybody she had been given anything. Sefu then says the gap in his own answer out loud, because a bell with nobody at it is a rope with nobody on the end of it, and the gap is closed by name rather than by silence. This is the only new canon in the batch and it is an addition to a refusal, not a subtraction from one.
- **B9 — Chapter 32's comparison was arithmetically impossible.** "the count … went from one to two, which was **more than** the Hearthholds' entire standing stock, which had been **six**." Two is not more than six. It is now a third of it, and the line earns its place by naming what the second board actually is. Related: Chapter 32 asked for "one of **the two** sound boards in the boundary work area" and Chapter 34 took "one sound board out of the boundary work area," but Chapter 26 called those the only two sound boards there and set them as the corridor's safe strip, which is to say under nine people's feet. Chapter 32 now asks for a sound board out of the household store that has never been in anything, and says plainly why it cannot have the corridor's two. Chapter 34's release follows Mikka's third out of the same store, and her release count — one in Chapter 26, one in Chapter 32, one in Chapter 34 — is now consistent across prose and state.
- **B10 — Chapter 40 fell on a day the school runs.** Chapter 40 opened "on a **Thursday**"; Chapter 35 fixed the pattern as "the school ran on Thursday and not on Friday," and Clause 3 forbids work on a day the school runs. The chapter now falls on "a day the school was not running, in the first hour," and the third term is **staged** rather than ignored: Jo reads it with a thumbnail under it, and the period is named for the first bell, which is the only hour Clause 3 leaves anybody. She says that last part out loud in front of the anteroom so the next period has to say it too. Marek's refusal to mark the stone also loses its stray "on a Thursday." The volume climax, the stair's condition, and the day's outcome are all untouched.
- **B11 — Chapter 38 asserted corrections it never staged.** "four of the nine were changed on the spot and two were struck" described five events; the chapter staged one change and one strike. The count is now **two changed and one struck**, and the second change is dramatised: Mikka adds *no work while the covers are being lifted*, with the reason that a person cannot be on the stair and lifting a cover, and if she is not on the stair then nobody is in that wash room, and Renn insists her own name goes beside it because the boundary's refusals are not going to be one-sided. It is now two staged changes and one staged strike, which is what the sentence says.

## Craft repairs applied

- **C20 — unattributed "he".** Chapter 40's "Two hands on that band would take it out in a morning," he said was unattributed two paragraphs before Marek spoke, in a close-third-Marek chapter, against Marek's own refusal to do that. The speaker is **Iven**, and the line now names what the band is to him — the only thing in the building he would put a hand to, and he is not going to, because Renn is not wrong and it is not his to be right about.
- **C21 — Tams's nine days had no on-page source.** Chapter 37 now has Jo ask him what happens at the end of the nine days, because she is writing the number and will not put a figure on a page with nothing behind it, and gets it written under his own words: a hand is looked at on the day, and if it does not open then it is a month and not nine days, said at the time and not before. Chapter 40's tally attributes the nine days to Oren, who named them when he took the lever. This is the fix the review asked for and it also gives Tams's refusal a resolution that is his rather than the room's.
- **C19 — signature vocabulary at roughly four times its earlier density.** "own hand" runs 21 times across Chapters 31–40 against 5 in 21–30 and "read it back" 12 against 1. The motif is the book's and it was not cut out. Two instances were rehandled where the read-back was not the point — Chapter 40's page is now read *because a page nobody has marked still has to be read twice*, and the plan's second reading is folded into a single paragraph with Sefu's own paragraph. The rest is left, because the density is a symptom of a batch that is largely people writing things down, and the next batch's four events are the opening of the school corner floor, the pump gallery, and the route, not a review cycle.
- **Two errors of the reviewer's own class, found while applying the fixes.** Chapter 40's reading of the page said "the two struck clauses in the words of the **women** who struck them," and one of them was struck by Sefu. Now "the people who struck them." And Chapter 40 referred to Dalla's rule as one she gave "in the twentieth day," a day the book never uses; it is now the twenty-fourth, the day Chapter 37 already gives her that rule.
