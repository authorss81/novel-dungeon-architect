# Batch Summary — Volume 01, Batch 0002

## Outcome

Chapters 11–20 are complete and reviewed. The six-person Public Works crew remains alive at the Hearthhold air-gate anteroom and boundary work area, but the batch turns a conditional pass into a limited relationship with the people who maintain the route. Nera’s air authority, Sefu’s civic veto, and the specific consent of Renn Vale, Dalla Sen, Jo Verrick, and Mikka Hale remain distinct. The crew accepts a public interim work sheet, a named next review, and a route that can be stopped, withdrawn, or closed. The route is neither public property nor a private claim.

## Movement

- Chapters 11–12 turn the first test's immediate aftermath into named work. Leena receives a dry cot and care access; Jo takes a trial pump shift; Oren and Tams carry the frame; Pell calls readings off the marked stone; Nera corrects Marek's air assumption and Jo's craft judgment corrects his plan; the first resident-maintained shift records Jo's half-day deferred tool-route cost.
- Chapter 13 centers the missing charter. The old inspection charter does not cover the service shaft or Hearthhold gate. Consent, withdrawal, labor cost, and no-ownership language remain unfinished, and Jo and Mikka refuse the language of beneficiaries and claimants outright.
- Chapter 14 grants only a named work period in the common kitchen, clinic/school corner, and boundary workbench. Sefu's two concrete limits and Mikka's objection both stand. The crew learns daily safety practices and receives work-area access, not citizenship.
- Chapters 15–16 make the guild order and ordinary work tangible. The surface incline is physically closed; the courier names unsigned Form 7-B as an open record; Iven's witness bond is suspended; the crew stops counting on guild rescue, parts, or records. A workday of meals, water discipline, tool repair, and scheduled resident labor changes the outsiders' role from guests to bounded temporary maintainers — and a pump interval closes short when Jo's own route runs long.
- Chapter 17 uses the wet mineral seam, pump housing, clinic return, and returning-stair pressure cycle to force an air reversal. The bypass shortcut fails. The revised route requires a landing watch, clinic-return watch, work-bell relay, latched hatch, and short attended pump intervals.
- Chapter 18 brings the returning stair into local civic argument. Marek names his three options and chooses to map it, warn the rooms, and close it. Renn's school/infirmary history conflicts with the guild record without solving the missing receiver. The stair is black-cord restricted and its condition recorded for later work.
- Chapter 19 makes the maintenance work legible and accepted as an interim agreement. Iven talks the pump sequence aloud so Dalla can write it in her own hand. The work sheet records pump steps, air limits, consent, stop authority, withdrawal, labor cost, no overnight running, no ownership, and a named review.
- Chapter 20 is the local climax. A pressure turn exposes a scored delivery seat and a check that has taken a set. The crew refuses forced deeper admission, weighs a costly retreat toward the flooding Cinder Steps, and chooses limited continuation through short attended intervals. Sefu reads the sheet aloud before anyone marks it. The pump, air gate, stair restriction, and guild order all affect the decision.

## Water and material handoff

- Water ledger is unchanged and explicitly explained: five liters from the collapse plus one separately sealed condensation liter. During a named work period the Hearthhold kitchen's provision is the crew's drinking supply, and the sealed liter is opened only if a shift overruns or a person is unwell. Hearthhold meals and small recovery cups are counted separately. The pump draws flooded sump water, not drinking water. The kitchen provision is an unpaid obligation the crew has not settled.
- The pump retains the split bronze-and-fiber gland seal and restored drive-dog key. Its delivery check still chatters under sediment, and the bronze delivery seat is now scored. The pump is limited to short attended intervals; no spare seat has been released; no overnight running is permitted.
- Marek has no formal power stage, bearing mark, or repeatable reading. His right hand has permanent fine-control loss; Leena's shoulder remains slinged; Pell's left ankle remains splinted. Oren and Tams remain alive and visible in labor, and Tams appears by name on the Chapter 16 work board.
- The returning stair has a visible movable slab, throat, rope, wheel, pressure-fed seam, counterweight, and missing lower receiver. A dead pack does not repeat the return; a walking human load does. It is restricted by black cord and a separate record, not resolved. In Chapter 18 Marek names his three options for it and chooses to map it, warn the rooms, and close it.
- The wrapped empty-seat plate remains on a spread of work cloth on the anteroom bench, still unexplained. It is not identified as the One-Seat or activated.
- Nera controls air testing and safe air-gate operation. Sefu independently controls civic admission. Form 7-B is still unsigned in Marrowgate and is named as the open record of who answers for the collapse. The old clearance seal, inspection charter, salvage lien, insurance notice, Dael's route-custody order, Iven's suspended-bond receipt, physical ownership, and Marek's non-claim receipt remain distinct.
- Isel Ferrow, a fevered Hearthhold resident in Chapter 17, is named and given an outcome in Chapter 19. Her cot is the reason the clinic cannot be emptied during an air reversal.

## Relationship and thematic change

Marek’s control flaw appears as a desire to fill every blank in a route agreement. Leena’s visible corrections and the residents’ refusal to sign away consent force him to leave uncertainty on the page. Nera remains an independent air engineer whose correction changes the plan rather than joining it. Iven’s guild choice now costs him a suspended witness bond without making him a full defector. Jo, Renn, Dalla, and Mikka make maintenance a set of relationships and costs rather than a free resource. Pell and Leena’s injuries remain present in every labor choice.
