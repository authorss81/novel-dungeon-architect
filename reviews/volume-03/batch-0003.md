# Review — Volume 03, Batch 0003 (Chapters 121–130)

**This is a self-review, run in the same session as the prose. It is not an independent review and it does not claim to be one.** **`reviews/volume-02/batch-0003.md`, `batch-0004.md` and `batch-0005.md` still do not exist and are owed to the reviewer's phase.** Batch 0002's record was corrected after an independent review found a class of defect the first pass had certified as clean; **the finding below about paragraph-index repairs is the same class, and this record is written so that Batch 0004 does not repeat it.**

## Scope and method

All ten chapters were read as delivered prose, then the batch was measured by the same method that reproduces the recorded figures for Batch 0002 exactly: paragraphs that do not begin with a quotation mark, taken in the raw text with the bold markers left in place so that a bolded narrative opener does not count; and a 90-character prefix for duplication. Word counts were taken per file. **Markup was checked separately, file by file, for an even asterisk count, a zero paragraph-level balance, no nested spans and no trailing run of three or more asterisks.**

**The method was validated against `chapters/volume-03/chapter-0111.md` through `chapter-0120.md` before it was used, and it returned Batch 0002's recorded figures exactly: 216 paragraphs not opening in dialogue, *The* 4, *Nobody* 3, *And* 2, *She* 10, *He* 20, thirty together; and on the bold-stripped measure *The* 36, *Nobody* 3, *And* 24, *She* 12, *He* 23.** The method is sound and the figures in this batch and in `state/batch-summary.md` are counted, not estimated.

## What the batch delivers against the brief

Every commitment in the batch prompt is delivered and bounded.

- **The week clock is exact and the arithmetic laid over it is exact.** 121–125 the twenty-fourth week and 126–130 the twenty-fifth, each chapter on the matching day of its week, the corridor shut in 121, 122, 124, 126, 127 and 129 and open in 123, 125, 128 and 130, the school on the three open days. **No day number is attached to any in-batch event, and the day-number register's highest value is unchanged at the forty-sixth day.** The only day figures left in the prose are the locked road, the arrangement's *three days in seven*, week figures in mouths, and the count in the column with no heading.
- **The reversal is the bible's own worked failure case and it is paid.** `bible/power-system.md`, under *Route change*: *a sealed door saves a leaking room from water but traps six people and cuts off the only ventilation shaft.* **The room holds. The structure is saved. The ventilation and the only exit are gone.** He chooses to close it and he is right; he says the alternative out loud and names both numbers and the higher one is on the other side of the door; he does not decide in a hurry and nobody accuses him of hurrying; he does not have the power to do it alone and is not asked to; **the door is closed by the person who keeps the room, on his recommendation, and that person is not to be blamed, and the rule that protects her is worth exactly not being the one a doctor looks at.**
- **The cost is people and it is counted honestly, and it is staged rather than narrated.** The count is read out at both ends of a corridor in the number eleven, the number is said before the name, and the named injured person is **Orla Kern, nine, the fourth of the nine**, with a foot that cannot take weight. **Mero Senn's rule is the volume's instrument and it is the volume's rule twice: said by him in Chapter 127 and said by a schoolteacher in a corridor in Chapter 126 who does not know whose it is.**
- **The count of the trapped and the first column of the plan are the same object.** The first column has been recording a permitted occupancy for twenty-five weeks; **a new row is written on the plan in the cook's hand with a ten in it that nobody counted, by copying the rule, and the room it describes held eleven; a boy of fourteen read it aloud in a room and said ten; and nobody in the building joins the two numbers and the book does not join them either.**
- **The professional engine and the civic engine both run, and neither is a fight.** The professional engine is the frame, the sill, the joint, the water, the room and the room's count. The civic engine is a reply sheet with a line for a keeper, a printed line headed for structures whose condition is kept by a person with three rooms under it, a form on a board at a gate, a title in a field book, and a market whose buyers do not live on it. **They collide in Chapter 122 and again in Chapter 130, and the collision is that a room people are being kept out of is a room nobody can say anything about from outside, and two sheets of paper left by people who were inside it in the dark are the argument.**
- **The aftermath is handed on.** **The argument about consent is made explicit in Chapter 130 in Renn Vale's and Iven Tallo's and Mikka Hale's own words: three offices have printed a line with a space in it for a person in this boundary, and a title is a box a letter goes into, and a sound sector does not get a crew put in it again.** Nobody is recruited and the batch says so.
- **The relationship guardrail holds and one of the volume's five acts is spent deliberately.** **Nera Oduya opposes Marek Venn in public, in a room with twelve people in it, on a matter that matters, and is right, and nobody consoled her, and she says out loud that she knows who is going to lose and that it is not she.** That is the third of the outline's five acts, and the batch spent it on the strongest available ground rather than drifting into it. **The other two were not attempted.** No intimacy, no recognition scene, no acceleration and no scene whose only content is the two of them. **Their closest moment in this batch is a working moment in front of other people in which she is right and he loses.**
- **The working practice holds in every record in the batch, and this batch is its proof and its limits.** The person who keeps a room writes the change to it; the schoolteacher writes the closure of her own room, the number in it and the reason the door is not wedged; the woman who keeps the terms writes the condition with no number and no day on it and says what the practice is worth. **The system worked exactly as designed and produced a trapped room, and the boundary read the trap out of its own rule in a corridor without anybody accusing anybody of anything.**
- **The people's guardrails hold.** The woman from the wash rooms is never named, asked, rostered or sent for, **her covers are struck out of a fourth instrument in nine seconds before anybody asked, and the reason is not given a fourth time**, and the floor is not approached. The ninth room is not sent for, the four treads are not walked, and **no mark is cut in the stone anywhere in this building.** Pell Roke is on the bench, reads numbers aloud, and does not cross the waist. **Talla Roke is not in this building and is not mentioned.** Iven Tallo is not redeemed, not a defector, not a martyr and not a saint, and he was not asked to sign anything. Oren Sill is not named. Dael Harrow is not named. Leena Orr's *a list is not a location* was deliberately not used again and the reason is recorded.

## Defects found and repaired

1. **Two chapters were over the length ceiling.** Chapters 128 and 130 came out of the writing session at 4,236 and 4,246 words against a ceiling of 4,000, and were cut to 3,895 and 3,981. The cut was of repeated restatement — the row-writing, the physician's speeches, the cook's reasons, the board, the closing catalogue — and no scene, beat or character was removed. **The batch is 34,370 words in all, 2,932 to 3,981 by chapter, and every chapter is under 4,000.**
2. **The connective tic was cut rather than managed, and this is the one place the batch is better than both batches before it.** The first pass carried **thirty-seven** bold `**And …**` section-divider openers, against Batch 0002's twenty-four and Batch 0001's forty-one. **Seventeen were rewritten into other shapes, which also took eleven plain `**The …**` openers out of the count.** The primary *And* count is **zero** and the bold-stripped *And* count is **twenty**. The lever is the one Batch 0001's reviewer named and Batch 0002 used: **cut the bold section dividers, not the plain openings.**
3. **A paragraph-index edit during the length pass introduced six duplicated paragraphs and dropped a paragraph and a speaker's line.** Two duplicates in Chapter 128 and four in Chapter 130, plus the loss of the bridge line *Nobody was frightened. And a cook asked him one question* in Chapter 128 and the loss of Marek Venn's entry into Chapter 130. **All six duplicates and both losses were found by recounting the files after the cuts and were repaired in the chapters, and the figures in this record and in `state/batch-summary.md` are the counted ones from the files.** **This is the defect class the Batch 0002 independent review found in a self-review, and the rule for Batch 0004 is that a batch must be recounted from the files after any structural edit, because a repair made by paragraph index will duplicate paragraphs and drop neighbours.**
4. **Eleven elapsed-day figures were removed or re-registered.** A count of days between two in-batch events is a date on an in-batch event whatever its arithmetic, and the batch prompt forbids it. The first pass had *three days and been not keeping it three days*, *looked for two days*, *thought about a number for two days*, *did not act on it for four days*, *spent four days since then*, *a room four days before it moved*, *a day late*, *the whole of this took four days*, *four days of work* by the survey, and *I was four days down this road at the second gate*. **Every one was replaced with a day of the week, a day of a named week, or the locked road, and the second gate's distance is stated nowhere in the batch.**
5. **Five further instances of meta language were cut** — *the reason this batch exists*, *the sentence this batch is built on*, *the reason this chapter is the shape it is*, *the reason this batch ends*, *the last person in this book*, *the shortest thing anybody has put in this book in twenty-four weeks*, *this book says where he is*, *the only protection a wage has ever had in this book*, and *for the fourth time in this book*. **The surviving uses of *in this book* were checked against the canon, which uses the phrase in Renn Vale's mouth in Chapter 117 and in the clerk's own entry in Chapter 118, and *in this Hearthhold* is now used for the batch's own new instance.**
6. **Marek Venn was named in six of ten chapters on first measure, including neither of the last two.** This was the batch's most serious craft failure and it was structural: the reversal's aftermath is carried by a schoolteacher, a cook, a clerk, a woman with a rule and a physician, and the man who recommended it had no interior life in four chapters including the two the prompt named. **Four beats were written and placed: the millimetre written in a private book at a first bell and given to a woman and not to a room (128); the week in which being right about five walls stopped being the same fact as being right about a floor and the entry that five is a number he chose and not a number he counted (128 and 130); the statement that he thinks being right about a series cost a boy his life and that he has nothing to offer about it (129); and the admission at the end of the batch that the sound in the room is not the anteroom and that he has no standing whatever to say what a sound in a house is (130). He is now named in ten of ten.**
7. **A pronoun and a number were wrong and were repaired.** Chapter 126's count line read *they counted him out* in a sentence about eleven people, nine of them children, and it now reads *they counted them out*. A repair made by index during the same pass also removed a closing paragraph from Chapter 130 and it was restored.
8. **Six markup defects were introduced and repaired.** Two unclosed bold spans, two paragraphs whose bold opening was lost when a duplicate was deleted, and two bold spans that had been left with a leading quote after a de-duplication. **All ten files are even on asterisks, every file's paragraph-level balance is zero, there is no nested span, and there is no run of three or more asterisks at the end of any paragraph.**

## Findings for Batch 0004, which are not defects in this batch

1. **The two unspent relationship acts are Nera Oduya's first and second** — *she stops checking his arithmetic out loud and somebody notices and she gives an answer about the work and not about him*, and *she countersigns his revision in her own hand on a page with an objection stated first* — **and one of them is owed in Batch 0004 and one in Batch 0005.** Her public opposition in this batch does not consume either of them.
2. **The reversal's own answer is the volume's next movement and it is available.** **The room that was sealed is kept open under a public occupancy plan, and the plan is the reversal's answer to itself.** The plan now has ten rows, a first column that is a permission, a fourth column that is empty with a reason in two hands, and a row that a room with nine children in it did not have six weeks ago. **Batch 0004's plan is that page and not a new document.**
3. **The chamber that has the most work in it is the count in the column with no heading.** **A dead man's count that the clerk has said out loud will not stop, a column with nothing over the top of it that now holds a room, and a boy's question about whether one becomes a rota because the woman who refused a rota has been dead for most of this book.** The prompt's own line — *a person is a person and not a row* — has never been sharper and Batch 0004 may use Leena Orr's sentence once, and Batch 0003 deliberately did not.
4. **The omission in Chapter 118 is untouched and must stay untouched.** The clerk had the chalk in her hand and did not put the four facts side by side. **No chapter of Batch 0003 undid it, explained it, or had anybody notice it, and the batch deliberately did not record a second omission**, so that the one entry remains the only one. **The two numbers, eleven and ten, are in different chapters in different hands on different instruments and nobody joins them, and Batch 0004 may not join them either.**
5. **A self-review that repairs prose by paragraph index will duplicate paragraphs.** Three of this batch's eight findings are that, and Batch 0002's independent review found a whole class of elapsed-time defect that its same-session review had certified as clean. **The rule for Batch 0004 is that the measured figures are recounted from the delivered files after every structural edit, and that a review which certifies a batch as exact is checked by somebody who did not write it.**
6. **Elapsed spans stay in the day's register.** A day number is a date and is load-bearing; a week figure in a mouth is a register and is not. The batch prompt's rules for this held and eleven first-pass figures broke them.
