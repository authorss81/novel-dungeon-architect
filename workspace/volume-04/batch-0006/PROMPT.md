## Craft — these are the four rules Batch 0005 was the first written under, and they are not optional

1. **No epigraph block and no closing recap block. A chapter opens on the room and closes on the room. The only bold in a chapter is a person speaking. If a paragraph of narration is bold, it is an error.** Batch 0005 delivered zero fully-bold paragraphs; Chapters 171–175 still carry 36 and that is a recorded divergence in already-delivered prose and not a licence.
2. **The request-to-speak tag is dead as a device. The half against is canon and must be in your chapters, and asking for it in the same four words is retired.** Batch 0005 ran **zero** request-to-speak openers and **two** bare *Say* imperatives in five chapters, in two different mouths, and the half against in five chapters in five different mouths with the literal four-word opener appearing twice and both times in one chapter. **A room asks in whatever shape the speaker has, and several answers arrive with nobody having asked.** The clerk's recitations are carried in **plain narration by default**, and the ones that stay speech are the ones that are acts.
3. **Quoted share is a real constraint. Every word inside matched quotation marks over total words. Batch 0005 ran 47.5 to 59.5 with a batch mean of 53.7. Land between roughly forty-five and sixty-five; a chapter above seventy is a chapter with no scene left in it.** Narration must carry the room, the objects, the weather of a working morning and the interior life.
4. **Pressure must come from a decision, not from a delivery.** A document may arrive; it may not be the cause. Batch 0005 turned all five chapters on somebody deciding something. **Seven chapters of one problem is exactly how a batch starts running on one kind of pressure, so name the pressure type of each chapter before you write it and make 188, 189 and 190 three different kinds.**

**And one number that is not a target: the 4000-word ceiling is retired.** It was invented in Batch 0003, is not in `AGENTS.md`, `NOVEL_SPEC.md`, `outline/volume-04.md` or the bible, and Batch 0005 delivered 3463, 3400, 4382, 3839 and 4705 with three of five over it and nothing trimmed. **Report lengths as measurements and do not trim delivered prose to hit a figure that was never canon.**
