# Chapter 146 — The First Time She Shut The Air Gate

**Shut for the corridor on the first day of the twenty-ninth week, a Monday, and the school did not run and did not lose anything, and the woman who keeps the road wrote a change to a room people use in her own hand with a condition and no number and no day on it, and the clerk read it back against the original and compared the two, and the corridor was open on a day the arrangement shuts it, and about thirty-one people of this Hearthhold moved through four rooms without an order on them.**

**An air engineer used a mouth in this building she has refused to certify for twenty-nine weeks, as a vent, for two hours, on her own authority, and said out loud in a room that it was not for anybody in that channel, and then she shut the air gate, which she has never done in twenty-nine weeks, and no person in this building had ever used that authority for anything at all.**

Renn Vale had the slate on her knee on the landing at the first bell and she said the reason out loud before she said the change, and the reason was that she is the person who keeps the corridor and the corridor is a room people use.

"**The arrangement for twenty-nine weeks has been that the corridor is shut on the first, second and fourth days and open on the third, fifth and sixth, and that the crossing is four at a time for four minutes in the pump's window with a bearer at the near lip, and that is mine and I wrote it and I am not taking it back.**"

"**And I am writing a change to it this morning, in my own hand, because I am the person who keeps that room, and the change is this. The corridor is open today. Not as an exception. Open, with the four and the four minutes and the bearer at the near lip, and the pump attended, and the same terms in every other respect.**"

"**And the condition is this and it has no number and no day on it, and I am going to write it before I say it and then I am going to read it out and I would like the clerk to read it out after me against the original and compare the two, because it is a term and not a favour.**"

"**The corridor is open on a day the arrangement shuts it while there is a person at the low end of it, and it is shut again the moment there is not. And I am the person who opens it and shuts it, and nobody else in this building can do either, and that is not new and I do not want it to be.**"

"**And the half of it that is against me. I am opening a corridor on a shut day with water standing on the fill at the waist and I have done that three times in twenty-nine weeks on a crossing for water and never once for people, and the reason I am doing it is that a man is in a channel at the low end of that corridor and a network that cannot be used on any day of the week is four rooms and a hope, and I have decided this week that I will carry the risk rather than the rule. I have said that in this room already and I am saying it again so that it is in the same mouth as the term.**"

Dalla Sen read the term back out of the book against what Renn Vale had written, and Renn Vale read it out again herself and compared the two, and they were the same, and the clerk entered that a corridor had been opened on a day the arrangement shuts it, and entered that no closure had been removed, and entered that the school does not run on the first, second and fourth days and did not run today and did not lose a day today, and entered that nobody in this room was to be told which of two facts is the independent one.

"**And the anteroom takes ten including one litter for no more than two hours,**" said Renn Vale, "**and I am about to put about thirty-one people through it in the course of this morning, in twelves and then the rest, and it is going to be over its number and I am the person who keeps its terms and I am saying so out loud before anybody is in it, and the clerk has entered the broken fifth of the five three times and I am not going to ask her to enter it a fourth. I am going to work under a condition that has been broken for a fortnight and I am telling the room that I know it and that I am doing it anyway and that nobody is to blame me, because the rule says the person who writes the change to a room is not to blame for it and I did not write this one.**"

---

**For the first time the network was used, and it was used the way a woman with a rule said it had to be used, and nobody at the front of it and nobody at the back.**

In the first hour the kitchen emptied into the room at the kitchen-line end, and that room emptied into the common landing, and the common landing emptied into the anteroom, and the anteroom took twelve and then eight and then eleven and then nine, and about a girl of nine sat down on the anteroom step with a strip of crate board round her foot, and a physician stood at the bottom of the nine steps and did not go up them, and Marek Venn was not in that anteroom and had not been since Friday afternoon and was under a corridor floor at the other end of this building.

"**I want the whole of the first crossing said out loud by the room and not by me,**" said Ilsa Norr. "**My nine went over the waist four at a time with a bearer at the near lip in the pump's window, and the last one out of that room was a woman of sixty-eight and not one of the nine, and she decided when the room was empty and not me and not Renn, and the room was empty when she said it was. I have been the person who keeps that room for twenty-nine weeks and this is the first time that a person who is not me has emptied it.**"

"**And there is no order on it. I have been listening for one all morning and there is not one. There is no first and there is no last except the last out, and a network with no order on it is a thing I did not think anybody in this building was capable of doing on the first morning they tried.**"

Talla Roke worked the middle of it with her three and said her terms before she touched anything, and her terms were about the road and not about the building.

"**I am not here about the water and I said that on the third day of last month and I am saying it again because it is a Monday and people get busy. I am here because a road is only a road if an ordinary person can use it, and I have got thirty-one of yours who have never once been an ordinary person of a road, and on the second day of this week I am going to take them up the market road as ordinary residents and there will be no order on that road and nobody at the front of it, and my three and I will be four people out of thirty-five and not a crew and not an escort and not a rescue.**"

"**And I have said the thing about my mark once in this room and I am not going to say it again, because it is in your clerk's book in my own words and it is not mine any more, it is the building's, and a sentence that is in a clerk's book can be read out to me again, and I would rather that than that it could be taken off me.**"

---

**At the third bell the water came up the channel at the low end of the corridor, and the air in the channel went out from under a man, and the air engineer shut the air gate, and it was the first time she had ever used that authority for anything.**

Nera Oduya had the valve board in her own hands at the second step and she had a piece of paper in her own writing on it, which is what an air engineer's sheet is in a building with no paper in it, and she read it out in a room with eleven people in it and she read it as an instruction and not as an opinion.

"**This is what I have written and it is what this morning is going to be, and I wrote it at the first bell on the fourth day of last month and I have not changed it, and the reason I have not changed it is that the moment I start rewriting an air sheet the morning it is going to be used, I am doing a different thing from the thing I do.**"

"**The pump runs attended from the second bell to the fifth and two people and I am one of them and that has not changed in twenty-nine weeks. The corridor is open on Renn's term and the anteroom's outer service door is packed with stone and clay and is a finger and a bit out of true and it is not going to be opened for anybody today, including for air. Which means that for about two hours this morning the only opening in the front of this building is the mouth in the school corner, and it is warm, and it goes into the throat rather than out of it, and I have not certified it and I have refused to certify it for twenty-nine weeks and I am going to open it as a vent at the second bell and hold it open until the fifth.**"

"**I want the whole of that said in a room. Not at a valve. In a room. A mouth that has never been certified is going to be the only vent in this Hearthhold for two hours this morning, and the reason it is that mouth and not another is that the other one is packed with clay, and the reason it is not certified is that I will not put a rate on it, and the reason I will not put a rate on it is that I do not have one, and I have been not having one for twenty-nine weeks and I have kept a watch on it every day of them.**"

"**And nobody in this room is to write down after today that the mouth is fine. It is not certified and it is not fine. It is open because the alternative is thirty-one people in a building with one opening in the front of it and a woman on a step who is not going to be told so by me at the second bell.**"

Two strokes into the pump's second run the water came, and it did not come up the corridor, which was the point, and it came up the channel, and Nera Oduya was on the second step and at the anteroom table and the whole of the room heard her say one sentence to herself and then say it out loud.

"**That is the air going out of the channel. Get him off that sill and get him up onto the floor and do not send anybody down after him.**"

"**And I am going to do the thing I have never done.**"

She went to the air gate. It is on this level and it is four steps from the anteroom table and it has a wheel on it that is turned by hand, and it had not been turned in twenty-nine weeks, and what it does is that it changes where the air in this level of this Hearthhold is allowed to go, and the only person in the building who may turn it is her, and she turned it.

"**I am shutting the air gate,**" she said, from the wheel, to a room. "**Not the door. The gate. The thing that lets the air at this end of this building go down into the lower level and out. I am shutting it for about a minute, and while it is shut the air at this end cannot leave by the bottom of the shaft, and it will sit at the top, and the top is where a man in that channel is, and the water coming up that channel has nowhere to push the air to.**"

"**And I am going to say the rest of it out loud because there are eleven people in this room and one of them has been in that channel since Friday and none of them is me. I am not doing this for him. I want that said by me before anybody in this room says it after me, because in twenty-nine weeks I have watched this building turn a professional act into a kindness four times and I have watched what it does to the next one.**"

"**I am doing it because thirty-one people are going through a corridor with a waist in it this morning and a man is at the bottom of the one place in this building where the air goes before the water does, and if the air leaves through the bottom of that shaft then the corridor loses its air to a channel instead of gaining it, and that is not a moral question. That is a floor and a hole and the direction of a thing.**"

"**And the fourth thing, which is the one I would like entered, and it is about the authority and not about the man. I have been the only person who may turn that wheel for twenty-nine weeks and I have never turned it, and I have said out loud in rooms that I keep a watch on the air and I have published no rate and I have certified nothing, and I want it entered in a clerk's book that the first time I used that authority it was to hold air at the top of a shaft for a minute while a person was in water under a corridor, and that I did not consult anybody, and that the reason I did not consult anybody is that the authority is mine and the minute was mine and if I had asked, a room would have told me about a man in a channel and I would have had to weigh a man against a floor, and I am not going to do that in a room with eleven people in it.**"

Shut for a minute and a bit, and then she opened it, and the pump came off its check, and at the low end of the corridor a man got himself off a sill with a lamp in his left hand and got himself up through a frame with no grate in it onto a floor, and two people were on the frame and they took his weight and they did not carry him, and Marek Venn stood up in the corridor and could not stand up in the corridor for a while, and nobody helped him and nobody said anything to him.

Dalla Sen entered all three of it — the mouth, the gate, and the minute — and entered the air engineer's own sentence about not doing it for a man, and entered that nobody had argued with her, and entered that a room had been told a professional fact and not been thanked for it.

---

**At the sixth bell the clerk entered the day's number and the total and did not read either, and the count was read, and the board was not walked, and the reason for both of those was the same and it was the best one anybody in that anteroom had heard all week.**

"**Three today and a hundred and fifty-four since the keeper signed, and the boundary is at two hundred and seventy-one and a quarter, and it adds by the day the work was done, and the two columns reconcile, and they have reconciled for twelve weeks. The crew are at sixty-three and a half.**"

"**And I entered those two and I am not going to read them out, and I am not going to read them out because a corridor was open this morning that the arrangement shuts, and a term of a woman's was put against an original at the first bell, and if a book can be put against an original in a room on this side of the waist then it can be put against an original on the other side of it, and I have spent twenty-nine weeks being the person who stops that happening, and I am not going to be the one who opened the door on the same morning.**"

"**The pot is ten. The jars are two. The water is on the fill at the waist and it has not been off it, and there is still no number for that fill in this building, and the water came up the channel at the low end at the third bell this morning and it went down the channel, because there was a man in it with a lamp, and I am entering that in the boundary's column and not in the crew's and I have already given the reason once today and I am not going to give it again.**"

"**The count. Wen Pask is eighty-one days and eighty-one nights into a count that is his own.**"

"**And I did not walk the board, and the reason is the same one and it is the real one. A board is walked by a person who has nothing else on them, and I have had a term, a mouth and an air gate on this book since the first bell, and I am not going to improve on that by saying it was a busy morning.**"

---

**On the second day of the twenty-ninth week the corridor is shut by the same arrangement, and the woman who keeps the road will be asked whether she will open it again, and she has not answered, and the answer she gave on Monday had no number and no day on it in it, and the only condition in it was a person at the low end of that corridor.**

**At the low end of that corridor there is a man who has been there since the fourth bell of Friday, and a lamp, and two flags and a length of the crew's own spent line, and about a foot and a half of air between a man's face and eleven feet of corridor that nobody in this building has ever put a load on, and the page in the clerk's book that says somebody stands there is still blank, and nobody has been asked, and nobody is going near that floor.**

**On Wednesday morning a woman from another office is going to sit in this anteroom and ask to be in the room while a page is read out loud, and the page has no name on it, and a courier is going to take thirty-one people up a market road with no order on it, and there is a form at the end of that road with two lines and a space in each of them.**
