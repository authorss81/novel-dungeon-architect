## Returning stair and evidence

The returning stair is restricted, corded, and recorded, and Batch 0004 has changed the crew's understanding of it without touching it. The guild's *receiver* is a **line-block**, not a seat: a hundred-year running groove in the bottom of the hollow, a bright four-inch band of unwetted stone on the front face above it, and a wedge-shaped third of a dressed block sitting in the gap as a **stop**. The counter-line was taken out in three sections. The guild's entry says *to works*; Renn's account says the works was a school, and a school register in a locked box says the stone was given to a wash door in the ninth week of the long cold year. Nobody crossed the slab, nobody crossed the cord, nobody put a hand past the line, and nobody cut a mark in the stone. The wrapped empty-seat plate rests on a spread of work cloth on the anteroom bench, unexplained, and nobody in the batch looked at it once.
