## How this batch was written, and what the review did to that

**A partial checkpoint draft of Chapters 101–108 existed in `workspace/volume-03/batch-0001/` when this batch was resumed, and it was replaced in full rather than continued.** The checkpoint commit is `db6555d novel: checkpoint batch-0001`.
