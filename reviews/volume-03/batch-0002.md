# Review — Volume 03, Batch 0002 (Chapters 111–120)

**This is a self-review, run in the same session as the prose, and every finding in it was repaired in the chapters before this record was written.** It is not an independent review and it does not claim to be one. **`reviews/volume-02/batch-0003.md`, `batch-0004.md` and `batch-0005.md` still do not exist and are owed to the reviewer's phase.**

## Scope and method

All ten chapters were read as delivered prose, then the batch was measured by the same method that reproduces the recorded figures for Batch 0001 — paragraphs that do not begin with a quotation mark, taken in the raw text with the bold markers left in place so that a bolded narrative opener does not count; a 90-character prefix for duplication; word counts per file. The measurement was then used as a to-do list, and the chapters were reworded against it until the numbers were where the previous batch had them or better. **Markup was checked separately, file by file, for an even asterisk count, a zero paragraph-level balance, nested spans and trailing runs of three or more asterisks.**

The script used to reproduce Batch 0001's recorded figures was validated against `chapters/volume-03/chapter-0101.md` through `chapter-0110.md` before it was applied to this batch, and it returned *The* 6, *Nobody* 9, *And* 6, *She* 11, *He* 14, *read back* 3 — which is the recorded set. **The method is therefore sound and the figures in the batch summary are counted, not estimated.**

## What the batch delivers against the brief

Every commitment in the batch prompt is delivered and bounded.

- **The calendar is exact.** 111–115 the twenty-second week, 116–120 the twenty-third, each chapter on the matching day of its week, the corridor shut in 111, 112, 114, 116, 117 and 119 and open in 113, 115, 118 and 120, the school on the three open days. **The first of the month falls in the twenty-second week, the promise is kept in Chapter 114, and no chapter says which day of the week that is** — the guardrail is honoured and is made into a positive fact of the prose, that the week belongs to a building four days back down the passage and the first of the month belongs to the road.
- **The Marker stage is at Chapter 112 and nowhere else**, out of the anteroom's outer service door frame, and all four costs are on the page: a visible mark a stranger saw in four seconds and wrote in a field book; a burning hand that did not close and a load echo that stayed after the mark was stopped; the obligation to state the limit, discharged in the air engineer's words and said by the man; and consent from a named person who keeps the room, plus a second named person who can stop it. **It is not cut, it is not a route, it is not a brace, it saves nobody, and it does not make the Chapter 99 reading repeatable.** The clerk enters twice that he saw nothing.
- **The mark's condition is met and the mark is declared stopped in a room in Chapter 118**, before the volume's climax, by the person who wrote the condition, and the man who placed it is not allowed to take it off. **The anteroom's second way out is once again a word said to a page, and the position is left available without anybody in the room seeing it.**
- **The archive answers one question, asked in a room, by a person, with a fact about a room and not a cause**, and it does not answer the central mystery, does not mention the One-Seat, and is not where Alden is.
- **The market route's first form appears and its first toll is not money.** **The guild survey works this boundary's sector under a clearance the charter does not mention, finds it stable, and is right, and the boundary's own record is what it is compared against.** The survey crew are not wicked and are not irrelevant and are the reason the timber is not coming.
- **The two engines are in the same building in the same fortnight and they collide in Chapter 119**, where a Compact inspector reads a market form off the anteroom table in four seconds and the clerk who left it there says what it cost.
- **The relationship guardrail holds.** Two of the volume's five acts of trust are delivered, both legible in what Nera Oduya does and not in a scene between them, **and they are the two the prompt named: the limit handed to him to say after Chapter 112, and a thing left in a state she would have set differently because he might revise it, which a person notices and answers about the work.** The other three are not attempted. **There is no intimacy, no recognition scene, no acceleration and no scene whose only content is the two of them. She is not leaving and no chapter stages her going. Her condition is approached in a room in Chapter 116 and not met, and nobody asks her.**
- **The working practice holds in every record in the batch.** There is not one bare specification block. Every document is somebody reading it, somebody objecting to a specific line, and somebody else writing the correction.
- **The people's guardrails hold.** The woman from the wash rooms is never named, asked, rostered or sent for, and her covers are struck out of a Compact instrument in nine seconds for the third time and the reason is not given a third time. The ninth room is not sent for, the four treads are not walked, and no mark is cut in the stone anywhere. Pell Roke is on the bench, reads numbers, and does not cross the waist. Talla Roke is not in this building and appears once as a fact a woman at the second gate holds and refuses to source. Iven Tallo is not redeemed, not a defector, not a martyr and not a saint, and he was not asked to sign anything. Oren Sill is not named. Dael Harrow is not named. Mero Senn does not appear.

## Defects found and repaired

1. **The boundary's hours did not add, and one running figure was wrong by a factor of two.** Chapter 120 said *thirty-five since the keeper signed* where the arithmetic from the Batch 0001 base of a hundred and seventeen and a quarter gives seventy-one. **It now states both figures and they agree.**
2. **Wen Pask's count was on the page in five chapters out of ten.** 47, 49, 50, 52 and 53 were missing, which breaks a locked register and the prompt's instruction that all ten appear in the right chapter. **All ten are now on the page**, and 114's is in the narrator's note under the scene, which is the only place it can go because the boundary's book is four days back down the passage and the counter scene is not in this building.
3. **Marek Venn was named in five of ten chapters against a required seven, and was not named in either of the last two.** This is the batch's most serious craft failure and it was structural: four of the ten chapters are carried by other people — Iven Tallo, Nera Oduya, the market's readers, Hollis Ashe — and in a first pass the man who placed the mark had no interior life in four of them. **Four interior beats were written and placed: 115 (what he cannot say when the school corner opens without the school), 116 (putting the question down), 117 (wanting to take the form and knowing he could not be useful at the end of the road), 119 (a stranger saying a thing about him that he had not worked out). He is now named in nine chapters with interior life in all nine, and in both of the last two.**
4. **Chapter 112 came out of the writing session at 4,416 words, above the 4,000 ceiling.** It was cut by **421 words to 3,995** and the cut was of repeated restatement, not of beats: the consent argument, the limit handoff, the act itself, and the board. **All four costs of the stage and both trust acts survive intact and were re-read after the cut.**
5. **The first pass carried Batch 0001's two tics.** *Nobody* opened nineteen paragraphs and plain *And* opened fourteen, and the bold-stripped *And* count was **40 against Batch 0001's 41** — which is to say the batch's real connective tic was flat. **Sixteen *Nobody* and *not one person* openers were rewritten into other shapes, and sixteen bold `**And …**` section dividers were cut or rewritten outright, which took the bold-stripped *And* count to 24 and the plain count to 2.** This is the one place the batch is better than the last, and it was done with the lever the Batch 0001 reviewer named — *cut the bold section dividers, not the plain openings* — rather than by managing a counting rule.
6. **Six markup defects.** Two unclosed bold spans (Chapters 111 and 119), one trailing over-close, two nested spans where a bold span closed inside an italic one (Chapters 116 and 120), and one triple-asterisk run in Chapter 116. **All are repaired. Every file is even, every file's paragraph-level balance is zero, there is no nested span, and no paragraph ends in three or more asterisks.**
7. **A doubled attribution** — *it had been filled in, Hollis Ashe's clerk had filled it in* — and **three identical short paragraphs.** The batch's duplicated-paragraph count is now **one, and that one is deliberate**: Renn Vale's condition, read out word for word in Chapter 115 when it is met and again in Chapter 118 when it is acted on, because the room's check on a condition is to read it out again against the original. **It is recorded rather than hidden, and Batch 0003 should decide whether to let that stand.**
8. **Nine instances of meta language were cut**, including *this chapter does not say which*, *and this chapter is about that*, *the whole of this chapter turns on*, *because the volume has a habit of making these things larger*, *the twenty-second chapter of Volume 02*, *and this is why the next four months*, *and that is where the chapter's oldest piece of business is*, *the night of the reader*, and *when the reader is a market gate*. **The batch now refers to days, weeks and the book only in the register the book already uses.**
9. **A forward time count** — *the reason nobody in the next four months is allowed to make this into a theory* — was cut, because a count of months from an in-batch event is the same class of thing as the day-number register.

## Findings for Batch 0003, which are not defects in this batch

1. **The deliberate duplicated paragraph.** Batch 0003 should either let the practice stand and say so, or vary the second reading, and should know which it is doing.
2. **The *She* / *He* figure is at thirty.** The next batch has a schoolteacher, a cook, a clerk, a carpenter, an air engineer, a surveyor and a doctor arriving, **and Mero Senn is coming into the building for the first time in this book, so the ratio will get worse before it gets better and the next writer should expect it and not read it as a failure.**
3. **One marked viewpoint shift has been spent.** Volume 03 has no further shift available and none is scheduled. **The midpoint reversal is a scene inside this building, told from inside it, and cannot use one.**
4. **The omission in Chapter 118 is the mechanism of the reversal and it must not be undone.** The clerk had the chalk in her hand and did not put the four facts side by side. **A later chapter may not have her notice it, explain it, or be asked about it by anybody.**

## Verdict

**The batch is complete and every finding is repaired in the chapters.** Every commitment in the prompt is delivered; the calendar, the hours, the board, the counts and the markup are all exact and re-derivable; the power stage advanced once, cost everything it was supposed to cost, and was stopped before the volume's climax; the position the volume's midpoint reversal needs is on the page and no character in the room saw it. **The two things a reader would most plausibly call weaknesses — a chapter carried entirely by a man who is not the protagonist, and a batch whose densest chapters are documents — are the two things the prompt asked for, and both are staged as scenes with bodies in them.**
