**This is a self-review, run in the same session as the prose. It is not an independent review and it does not claim to be one.** `reviews/volume-02/batch-0003.md`, `batch-0004.md` and `batch-0005.md` do not exist for a closed canon volume; **independent reviews of Volume 03 Batches 0003 and 0004 are owed because the files that exist disclaim independence in their own first lines, and an independent review of this batch is owed on the same grounds.** Batch 0004's review found a class of defect its own first pass had certified as clean — markup — and found a second class it had certified without ever measuring — viewpoint presence. **Both classes appeared in this batch again, and the finding about that is the most important thing in this record.**

## Scope and method

All ten chapters were read as delivered prose and then measured by the method that reproduces Batch 0004's recorded figures exactly, with its exclusions stated: a paragraph is a block of the raw file split on blank lines; a paragraph opening with a quotation mark is dialogue; **the ten `# Chapter NNN` title lines are not paragraphs; the `---` section rules are counted as non-bold non-dialogue paragraphs rather than dropped; and the duplication check runs on content paragraphs, not on bold section-divider paragraphs, because this house uses a recurring divider formula for the sixth-bell read.** Bold markers are left in place. Word counts are raw `wc -w`. Markup was checked file by file for asterisk evenness, quotation evenness, paragraph-level quotation balance, trailing asterisk runs and duplicated paragraphs.

**The method was validated against `chapters/volume-03/chapter-0131.md` through `chapter-0140.md` before it was used, and it returned Batch 0004's recorded figures exactly on twenty of the twenty-one measures: 207 / 129 / 78, raw 217 / 139 / 78, including-bold openers *The* 4, *Nobody* 1, *And* 11, *She* 7, *He* 3, excluding-bold *The* 2, *Nobody* 1, *And* 3, *She* 5, *He* 3, *read back* 4 and *read it back* 1, and lengths 2,598 to 3,891 and 31,228 in all.** The twenty-first was the duplication count, which returns 2 on the raw measure for a batch whose recorded figure is 0, **and the reason is the third exclusion, and that reason is now part of the method rather than a footnote, because a figure that cannot be reproduced by the next writer is a figure that will be handed forward as validated and will not be.** The method is sound.

## Defects found and repaired

1. **The speech-paragraph markup form was wrong on delivery in five files, and two automated passes over the ten files were both wrong, and the batch's own lesson from Batch 0004 was relearned the hard way.** **The delivered form of this book is a quotation mark, then two asterisks, then the text, then a closing quotation mark, with no closing pair of asterisks.** This batch had been written the other way round in places: forty-eight paragraphs carried a closing pair of asterisks and no closing quotation mark, and a number carried a closing quotation mark and no pair. **A first normalisation pass stripped a closing pair from the wrong paragraphs and inverted the form in five files, and a second pass left two bold dividers with a stray closing quotation mark and two paragraphs with an unbalanced quotation count.** All ten files were put into the single delivered form and then verified file by file. **No prose was reworded by either pass; only quotation marks and asterisks moved.** Batch 0004's review wrote that a batch which repairs markup by script will break prose and that only recounting the files finds it. **That was written about Batch 0004. It happened again in Batch 0005, in the same repository, in the same week of work, and the rule belongs in the outline rather than in a review file.**

## Verdict

**As delivered, every commitment in the Batch 0005 prompt is met, and the volume's ten commitments from `outline/series.md` are all delivered across the five batches.** The volume's immediate promise is answered: the room that was shut is open, under a public plan, public and bounded, with a person named in a room who may stop it. **The volume's larger question is asked out loud twice and is not answered either time, and the second asking is the better one because it has thirty-one people on the answer.** The cost is permanent and cumulative and nobody is forgiven for it: a right hand that has not closed since the nineteenth week, a left hand that closed on a lamp in a channel and did not shut afterwards, a mark on a forearm, a knee, a load echo, a public failure in a book of his own, a condition broken in a clerk's book and a page that is still blank, and a statement made in a room that he cannot go down there again and does not know why.

**The three things a reader would most plausibly call weaknesses are that the batch's two documents defeat themselves — the plan becomes public because a stranger wrote a procedure saying a page could be shown, and a guild's form comes off a board because the guild's own rule could not use a courier's mark — that Chapter 144 is twenty-one per cent longer than the longest chapter of the previous batch, and that the volume ends on a question asked twice with nobody in the room willing to answer it. The first is the book. The second is a stated decision with a reason. The third is the volume's contract with the next one, and it was paid.**

**The failure this record has to name is its own, and it is the same failure Batch 0004's record named.** It had a craft baseline handed to it with a viewpoint-presence check in it and did not run the check, and the batch shipped with the protagonist absent from three chapters; and it relearned the markup failure the previous batch's review had already written down. **Two batches in a row have shipped with a defect that the previous batch's own review predicted in the exact words it would occur in, and both times the defect was caught by a final recount rather than by the process. That is the finding a Volume 04 prompt has to act on: the baseline is not a document to be read, it is a set of counts to be run before the state files are written, and a count that is written down from memory is not a count.**
