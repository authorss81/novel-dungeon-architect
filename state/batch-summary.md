**The volume's question is now three objects and no answer.** The oldest book in this building keeps nine rooms with a count and a condition and **not one name anywhere**, and it has a row at the bottom of four unwalked treads with a one in it, a *none* beside it and nothing at all under it, and it has **no air on it at all**. The newest book has a name against almost everything including a name against a refusal and it can be picked up by a stranger. And the book this batch made has a count and no name, a condition and no day, three lines of work and six that are empty, a fourth column that is empty and unheaded, **no name anywhere on it and a date on it** — and it is the only one of the three that cannot be taken out of the room it is about, because the man holding it sleeps upstairs of the clinic return and the only road out of this building is up nine steps and a gate. **Bel Ordry says the safest of them is the one with the most empty in it, and Nera Oduya says that will not survive the boundary's first year and should be suspected in about a month.** That is Volume 02's question converted into a permanent argument and it is not answered.

**The midpoint's fee has been paid and the price is a road.** The dependency was physical, the fee was the word *maintainer*, and the instrument that could have been used to charge a boy a year of his trade turned out to need **a named holder entered against a named route in a register kept by the office**, and this boundary has one route and it is the only object of care here a stranger cannot pick up. So the boy is not paid, the carpenter is not redeemed, the guild is not defeated, and **the thing that came back up the road is a woman behind a counter telling a competent office what it did, and the answer is that the office has halved the water.** The release cost the only *yes* this Hearthhold ever had; **it cost no day of the cook's count; and it cost half the water, and that last one nobody in this building saw coming, and the man who saw it coming was a woman at a counter who has been filing documents for nineteen years and was not asked to sit down once.**

**Iven Tallo's arc turns again and is not redeemed.** He refused a bond in a full room, argued for the charter that bond would have come from, said both were the same sentence, and then a boy of twenty-two was suspended by an entry in a roll and brought the sentence up nine steps on his back. In this batch the man who taught it him named the one instrument in this building that could give the boy his trade back, explained exactly what it would cost, and **refused it on the ground that he does not know whether he would still say no in the spring** — which is not a principle and is not the argument, and is the most honest thing he has said in thirteen weeks.

**The slow-burn stance held for a fourth batch.** No intimacy, no recognition scene, no acceleration. **Their closest moment in this batch is not between them either**: it is Nera Oduya walking as far as the turn and stopping, because a man who is asked to witness an asking is a different thing from a man standing in it, and then standing on a landing while two people she has opinions about have a conversation she cannot hear the argument of.

**No power stage was granted and none was warmed up.** Marek Venn has **nine written nulls and nothing usable — six in the ordinary column and three in the column with no heading, which is nine in all**, and the ninth was written in the ordinary column and moved by the count, and the count said so out loud: *a column with two in it is a column, and a column with three in it is a column, and I am not going to head it.* No bearing mark, no route change, no binding, no relay, no automatic solution, no rate, no view of the unaccounted air. The brass rule has said nothing for **eight weeks**. The wrapped plate is named, unidentified, unopened, and joined to nothing. **Nobody goes down the four treads and the eighth term stands.**
