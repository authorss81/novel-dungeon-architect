## Findings

1. **The batch is nine days of one building and it is the strongest thing about it.** Every chapter is the same room on a working morning with the bells, the terms, the pump, the pot, the frame, the wall, the sixth bell and the seventh bell, and the pressure comes from a decision each time and never from an event arriving. **The risk of a book that has run this shape for two hundred and ten chapters is that the shape becomes the point, and the counter-measure in this batch is the four days when the pump is off and the building is learning to do without a resource it has had for nineteen weeks. That is a different kind of morning and it is not louder, and it is the best material in the batch.**
2. **The strongest chapter is 206 and the reason is a person who is not in the story's standing cast.** Petra Wend walks up nine steps, refuses a chair and gives her reason before anybody can make a thing of it, and says the sentence the volume is built on, and the book does not correct her and does not let the boundary accept her either. **She is not grateful, she is not thanked, she is not a plot device, and she is not on the board, and a later phase must not make her one of the three.**
3. **The weakest passage is the third of the six terms.** It is now a standing fact that the water-carrying is false, and it is said every morning in all ten chapters, and by 208 the word *false* has stopped meaning anything to the reader in the way a number said out loud stopped meaning anything to the cook. **That is the intended effect and it is also a cost, and a later phase should either change the term or find a morning where the saying of it is the event.**
4. **The best single beat is the eighth refusal.** Tams Quill has refused seven times with a reason for six, and the eighth is the first that is not about a man refusing, and the eighth is the first time he has been asked to refuse a shape, and his reason — that the difference between a line on a board and a plan is not a heading, it is a corner — is the best sentence anyone says in fifty chapters of this subject and it is in a mouth that has spent eight weeks saying nothing.
5. **The batch's honest failure is that the Bracer does nothing in it.** The brace over the outer service door frame is standing, its condition is met, and this batch never once puts a body under it, because a batch about water in a gallery has no business standing a brace on its own frame for a scenic reason. **The stage is not re-granted and is not re-described and the cost is deferred to Batch 0004 by the volume outline, and this review's judgement is that the deferral is correct and that Volume 05 must now pay for it with nineteen weeks of watching rather than with a second build.**
