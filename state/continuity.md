That is what the crew is for, and it is the sting in the chapter: the first work anybody has asked the crew for in three weeks is bookwork about somebody else's work.

**The maintenance obligation is bounded and written and held by five, and its end condition — *until the boundary can do it without us and a person named by the boundary has said so out loud, in the room, with the reason* — has NOT been met.** **Sefu Aran, in Chapter 80, said *not yet*, in his own hand, and asked for it to be entered with a condition instead of a date: the first day of the month the pot stops falling.** **Two men have declined to end it, one on a figure and one on a judgement, and the two refusals are in the book next to each other on purpose.** The spring conversation happened in the twelfth week and produced one thing that is not in the ledger: **a name Nera Oduya said in her own hand, which the person has not been told, and which this batch has her refuse, in a room, to have anybody ask about.**

## Power and relationship guardrails

Marek Venn is still at the ordinary-engineering/unschooled-eye level. **Eight written nulls, all written down, all giving nothing usable — six in the ordinary column and two in a column with no heading — and no power stage was reached or hinted at in this batch.** He is corrected less often than in Batch 0001 and is corrected **by himself, twice**: in Chapter 71, that being sent out of rooms is a thing he had turned into a virtue; and in Chapter 75, standing in a room where a boy has been suspended by an entry in a roll, he understood that the danger was never a name on a page but a **date and a sentence and a stamp forty days away**, and he said none of it and did not write it on a slate. He asks out loud and is willing to be told no, and in Chapter 79 he did the whole of it — door, terms, request, refusal — and did not try once to make the no into something else.

**No viewpoint shift in the batch.** Chapter 58 was Batch 0001's single shift. **Batches 0002 and 0003 are two batches' work without one, and a third shift inside a thirty-chapter window would have had to earn itself three times over.** Nera's cold, her refusal, her misreading of the plan and her refusal to be asked about the hand-over are all carried in her own mouth and in what other people do about it. Recorded as an author decision.

**The no-bare-specification rule held.** Every record in Chapters 71–80 is staged as somebody reading it, somebody objecting to a specific line, and somebody else writing the correction in their own hand: the terms, the description of nine rows, the letters, the door's report, the guild notice, the clerk's assessment, the two columns, the pails, the release, and the last line of the book. **There is not one specification block in the batch, and the longest stretch of written matter is the clerk's assessment in Chapter 76, which is read out loud, objected to by the keeper, entered in the clerk's own hand, and read out again.**

**The working practice held and produced the batch's work:** any change to a room people use is written by the person who will keep it. **Nine new instruments in nine hands in Batch 0003, and not one of them a document anybody outside this building can hold — except the last one, which is deliberately the exception, and which the boundary named and priced and read out three times before it wrote it.**

**Slow-burn relationship policy held.** No intimacy, no recognition scene, no acceleration between Marek and Nera. **Their closest moment in this batch is not between them either**: it is Nera standing at the top of three steps saying that nobody in the room can tell which of two books is safer, and asking for it to be written so that she can read it out in a year and be believed.
