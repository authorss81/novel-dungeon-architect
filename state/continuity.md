Dael Harrow's route-custody order locks the guild-controlled surface door and freezes guild-financed equipment; it cannot open the Hearthhold gate, order air, create consent, or establish ownership. **Iven's Chapter 38 warning is new canon:** a stair is a route, a route is the only thing in this building the guilds have ever had a legal instrument for, and putting the lower side back into service is how a route charter arrives. The restriction on the stair therefore protects the crew as well as the rooms, and the crew should be aware of that.
