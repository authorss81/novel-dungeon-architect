## Immediate obligation for Volume 02, Batch 0003

- **OT-55 (partly discharged, and the batch's own escalation, now delivered).** **A route closure cuts off water, and guild workers and residents disagree over whether the fault is repaired locally or managed by a charter.** Frost took a set in the corridor's washed-out fill at the waist; the thaw let it go; **the ground slumped a hand's breadth twice in three days and the third wedge came up a quarter of an inch**, which is a wedge doing its only job. The corridor shut, and the water — which comes up the middle passage in pails on the shoulders of whoever is coming, and has to pass two two-foot-eight places to reach the kitchen — **stopped crossing.** The kitchen's count went **nineteen to sixteen to fifteen**, in Mikka Hale's own words, read out at the fifth bell every day. Then the room argued it out on the common landing **in the gaps between school crossings**, with Iven Tallo arguing **for** a charter on the ninth day of the batch after refusing a guild bond on the fourth, Nera Oduya pointing out that a charter buys the *pump* argument and leaves the *floor* argument exactly where it is, Sefu Aran refusing to let a keeper's wet-seam position become the building's timetable, and Mikka Hale saying that **a charter does not carry pails.** **Nobody chose. The choice is still open and the waist is still not packed.**
- **OT-56 (new, and dated).** **The register boxes and the plan on a dowel.** **The key turns in the thirteenth week**, which is where Batch 0003 opens. The terms are Ilsa Norr's and they are already partly on the page: **the crew are not in the room**; Nera Oduya will be in it and has said she will be the only person there who cannot be trusted with what is on it; the room is the people who live here. **This must be staged from outside the room and must not answer the volume's question.** A plan that shows homes and not treasure is not an answer — it is a second, older instance of the same shape this boundary already lives in, and the volume's argument is that somebody built this place to be lived in and then forgot, and that the forgetting is the whole of the mystery. **Do not explain the Cairn material, do not connect the plate to anything, and do not let the plan resolve anything.**
- **OT-57 (new, and it is the batch's best unresolved thing).** **Renn Vale's walk to the wash-room door.** She has promised, in front of Dalla Sen and the room, that when she does it, it will be her, at the door, she will stand on the step while the woman from the wash rooms says whatever she is going to say **including no**, and that **the whole of the room will be told what she said and none of the room will be in it.** She has said she will **write the day down herself and not be given one by anybody**, because the last time somebody gave her a date for a thing she had refused to do, she did it in a hurry and it cost a person a morning. **The thing in that room is the rack, which has a piece of wood on it that has been damp for thirty years, and it is the only piece of wood in this Hearthhold that a delivery seat could sit in and stay put.** The wash room floor is refused and stays refused. Renn's refusal of crew access to the woman is not withdrawn and was honoured absolutely through Batch 0002.
- **OT-58 (new, and it has been open for two batches).** **The blank on the review page.** *Somebody stands at the low end of the corridor and watches the water in the six minutes after the lever comes off, and there is nobody standing there.* Put there by Nera Oduya at the short review, and she **refused to name a person and said she would not**, because naming one in a week when the water came up is how a person ends up named in a worse week. **Dalla Sen reads the line out at the fifth bell every day with a blank against it.** The line is chalked on the pump frame and the frame is in the anteroom. **Chapter 70's last line moved this without closing it:** Marek Venn asked **Jo Verrick**, out loud, in the corridor outside the pump room, who was going to stand at the low end in the six minutes, and she considered not answering a question that was not hers to refuse, and said a name, and then did not say it, and then said it. **So the page is still blank, the name is in one woman's mouth and one man's ears and nowhere else, and the person has not been asked. The next batch must not fix the name without the asking, and the asking is the point.** Note that this is a *third* unnamed-name thread and it is distinct from OT-60: OT-60 is a name Nera Oduya said in a room about the air valve hand-over, and this one is a name Jo Verrick said in a corridor about the six minutes on the review page.
- **OT-59 (new, and it is the volume's question as an agenda).** ***Who keeps the Hearthhold rooms on the day there is no work left to do in them, and who notices, and how does anybody find out.*** Asked by **Mikka Hale** in the anteroom in the eleventh week, in a harder form than Ilsa Norr's, and **not answered**. The finding underneath it: a room is kept by the work in it, and a job is a reason for a person to be in a room, and when the work stops there is no reason for anybody to be in the room and nothing is missing. **And the batch produced a structural answer that is the wrong shape of answer and everybody can see it: the boundary's keeping has become Renn Vale's four days in seven, and its school has become Ilsa Norr's four days in seven, and a person is a system when there is nothing else.** Mikka's answer is **a book of favours in her own hand** — every person who carries water up nine steps for this Hearthhold unpaid, with the date, the name and the pails, read out at the fifth bell on Fridays — and she said why: *a favour is a loan with no date on it.*
- **OT-60 (new).** **A name Nera Oduya said in a room and did not put in the book.** The spring hand-over conversation happened on the second day of the twelfth week, the fourth bell, one hour, **Sefu Aran and Nera Oduya with the five of them and Dalla Sen, and nobody else**, terms named on the day, and the leaving not mentioned once by anybody. - **OT-61 (new).** **The delivery seat.** Named by the keeper and **not made**. The housing was opened, the seat was held up in a lamp in front of nine people, the bolt-hole at the back had opened a quarter of an inch, and the seat went back in. **Jo Verrick's estimate has moved for the first time in eleven weeks, in her own hand, from two or fewer to one**, and she has refused to write zero on the grounds that zero is a decision and a decision gets made on a day with a man standing in front of you. **The check is a hiss on one note and the two-note vocabulary is withdrawn**, because a hole hisses thinner in cold air; the pump runs attended, two people, second bell to fifth, Isel Ferrow and Jo Verrick, with Mikka Hale covering half the shutter route; and the draw is stopped by hand on **a rising water line chalked on the frame by Leena Orr**, because the water is the only instrument left. **There is no instrument, there is one cleaning, and there is no seasoned hardwood in this Hearthhold that is not somebody's.**
- **OT-62 (new, and it is the batch's real work product).** **Nera Oduya's hardwood inventory.** Twelve lines, every one of them **no**, and a thirteenth line under them that says what the seat wants and has **no column on that page for a person who has it**. It is a permanent civic object in the air engineer's own hand, it was taken apart by nine people over an hour and forty minutes, and four of the owners corrected their own entries — Ilsa Norr's from *Ilsa Norr's* to **the school, held by Ilsa Norr**; Bel Ordry's to *given, not available this month, and not to be thanked for*; and two dead sticks entered as dead sticks. **It should be extended, not replaced, and it is the instrument by which a Hearthhold argues about scarcity instead of about owners.**
- **OT-63 (new).** **The tick in the throat's north-east cheek.** Bel Ordry's bar, at the second bell, on a cold morning, gave three ticks with a slide on the third — the same shape as the wooden knock Nera Oduya heard from eleven feet down in the tenth week — **and the same tick in two other places on the same wall where he knows the stone.** Nera refused to write it as a knock and refused to write it as nothing, and entered the shape, the cold, the three places, and no classification in either direction, and said out loud that **the instrument had stopped being an instrument.** **The two knocks of Chapter 58 and the Chapter 60 sounding and this third sound are three separate events in three hands and must stay three.** Nobody has been down there. Nothing has been identified.

## Ending guardrails

- Morrowlight is a provisional network from Volume 11 and a durable self-governing city only after the final resolution.
