## The batch goal, as planned

**Volume 03 Batch 0001, Chapters 101–110, the twentieth and twenty-first weeks.** Open Volume 03 on the delivered canon, not on the series outline's fragile charter. **The boundary can just about keep itself, which means it has every reason to keep doing the cheap thing, and the cheap thing is a closure.**

Nine children have nowhere to go but across a fill that has moved three times and is not measured, on purpose, by three people. **The school corner is then shut as a room — a correct, cheap, competent closure that costs nothing, strands nobody, closes no corridor on any day of the week, and changes what the boundary is.** A competent Stonelight inspector arrives on a lawful permit from a line above the Cinder Steps **that this boundary did not know was there**, and the charter does not cover her, and the boundary has to invent a procedure for a body it never wrote one for — **and anything it invents is a document a stranger can use.** The keeper goes up four days of road to keep a promise he made out loud, **the boundary runs the rest of the batch without its elected keeper and discovers that it runs perfectly well, and his two true sentences are read out in a room in this building so that the people who would have to act on them know what they are.** The charter is amended by a **second sheet** that names a fourth object of care, because a wall with water in it has joined a stair, a lip and a valve.
