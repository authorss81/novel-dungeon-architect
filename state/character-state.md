### Marek Venn

- **Position:** the Hearthhold air-gate anteroom and its lower level, the common landing, and — by named work period and named terms — the lower corridor, the school corner, and the stair's service bend, fixed first landing, and near landing. **He is not on the rota and cannot stand a watch.**

- **What he has worked out, and it is not insight:** that the one sentence he came down a shaft to learn is *may I have your afternoon*, and that the thirteenth week taught him the harder one, which is *what is it that you already know how to do that nobody has thought to ask you about*. **Nobody had thought to ask the woman from the wash rooms that in eleven weeks.**

## The stair, the plan, the plate, and the evidence

The stair is **restricted, corded, and in service on its near side only**, with two timber shores on the common landing, the newer one packed and **not wedged**, and a lip that ranges **a finger and a half to a finger and three-quarters** in the cold, worse on a cold morning. **Four treads go down behind the rail on the far side and nobody has been on them and they are not walked this month — and a nine-hundred-year-old document has located a room at the bottom of them, and the boundary has refused to keep it by crossing it.** The far wall of the throat is eleven feet down and has never been seen. **Bel Ordry's cold bar has now produced six mornings: three nothings and three of a thing with a slide on the third, and the same tick in two other places on the same cold wall** — and a third slump of the waist's fill happened on the same Sunday as the third unclassifiable tick, and **the two facts are on the same page with a date on each and nothing between them, on purpose, and the reason for putting them near each other is on the page so that a reader in a year cannot make it into a finding.**

The **brass survey rule** is in the pocket of the coat on its hook and has said nothing for **seven weeks**, and Marek has not put his hand on it once in a fortnight.
