## Relationship, thematic, and power change

**The volume's argument is now two objects rather than one.** The oldest book in this building keeps nine rooms with a count and a condition and **not one name anywhere on the page**, and it has a row for a room that is kept on the condition *none*, and that row is at the bottom of a stair this Hearthhold has been crossing six times a week without knowing that was a keeping. The newest book in this building has a name against almost everything, including a name against a refusal, and it is thirteen days old and in a clerk's hand and can be picked up by a stranger. **Ilsa Norr says she cannot tell which of those is safer, and Nera Oduya says that nobody can, and asks for that to be written at the fifth bell so she can read it out in a year and be believed.** That is Volume 02's question converted from a question into a permanent argument, which is what the second half of a volume is for, and it is not answered.

**The midpoint landed.** A public thing — six crossings, a pot, a stair, a school, a clinic — was kept by people who receive it and by nobody else, and the first thing it cost was a man's shutter route and a day of a cook's count, and the second thing it costs is **a book**, and the word that named it was written by the best person in the building at writing and she did not know she was writing it. **Nobody was a hero in this and nobody was a villain, and the beneficiary and the maintainer are the same nine names in the same hands, and Renn Vale said so and then said *and that is not a scandal, it is a public thing being kept by the public, and it turns out the careful part is the danger.*** That is the batch's thesis and it is the volume's argument, and it is the reason the volume's answer is not available.

**Iven Tallo's arc turns again and is not redeemed.** He refused a bond in a full room, argued for the charter that bond would have come from, said both were the same sentence, and then in Chapter 75 a boy of twenty-two was suspended by an entry in a roll and brought the sentence up nine steps on his back, and the man who taught it him said *the argument holds* and refused to be forgiven and refused the boy's wish to work here unpaid. **He is not a defector and he is not a saint, and the batch's debt to him is unpaid on purpose.**

**Bel Ordry's refusal to be thanked is answered by the boundary acting on it.** The book of favours is a favour book that has become a rota, and the rota's *when* is a woman's boots, and Mikka Hale said out loud that she built it, and Renn Vale said the condition in a room has stopped meaning the weather and started meaning a rota, and a day the condition stops meaning the weather and starts meaning a person is the day this boundary begins to be kept by the people it is keeping.

**No power stage is granted.** Marek has eight written nulls and nothing usable, seven in the ordinary column and two in the column with no heading, which is eight in all, and every one of the eight is in the book. No bearing mark, no route change, no binding, no relay, and no automatic solution exists anywhere in the batch. The route, the water, the check, the waist, the school, the wash rooms and the ninth line are all worked by people who live in the building, and the only hours the crew spent in the fourteenth week were two, on a named work, asked for by name.
