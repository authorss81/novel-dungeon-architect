# Volume 01 Batch 0002 Outline

## Status: record, not forward plan

This file was written **after** Chapters 11–20 were delivered, and every card ends with a "Delivered" line. It is a record of what the batch was asked to do and what it actually did, kept so the state files and the next batch prompt can be checked against the intended cards. It is **not** the forward card set for a future batch, and it must not be used as one. The live forward plan for the next phase is `workspace/volume-01/batch-0003/PROMPT.md`; the forward plan for later batches is written when each batch prompt is created.

Where this record and the delivered prose disagree, the delivered prose is canon and this file should be corrected, not the reverse. Two such corrections were made during the review repair pass and are noted in `reviews/volume-01/batch-0002.md`:

- The Chapter 11 card said Renn and Mikka were on the landing and uncounted. The delivered chapter has Renn on the landing and Mikka at the sill, both uncounted, because she hands the recovery cup across the stone rather than carrying it in.
- The Chapter 17 card said the anteroom was cleared to the upper landing "by the rule written the previous day." No such written rule exists before Chapter 19. The clearance in Chapter 17 is the air condition Nera and Sefu set in the scene; it is written onto the sheet only in Chapter 19, drafted and unmarked, and confirmed in Chapter 20.

## Batch identity

- **Batch:** 0002
- **Chapter range:** 11–20
- **POV:** Close third person on Marek Venn. No further Nera viewpoint shift in this batch; the air work she performs in Chapter 17 is shared with Marek, so it gives her no interior knowledge the reader does not already hold.
- **Batch goal:** Turn the Chapter 10 conditional anteroom pass into a real but limited relationship with the people who maintain the route. The crew learns who keeps the water down, what residents accept as admission, and why a public route needs consent and a maintenance charter that no outsider can write alone.
- **Starting condition:** The anteroom has passed a conditional twelve-occupant test and carries a working rating of ten people including one stretcher for no more than two hours. The pump is temporary and attended. The guild surface door is locked. The deeper Hearthhold gate is closed to new admission.
- **Starting relationships:** Nera holds technical air authority, Sefu holds independent civic veto, and Renn Vale, Dalla Sen, Jo Verrick, and Mikka Hale have each volunteered for a specific part of one test and nothing more. Iven's maintenance signature has already cost him his Cartwright witness bond.
- **Batch midpoint:** Chapter 15–16. The guild line becomes a physical fact rather than paper, and the batch then deliberately drops into an ordinary workday, because a civic claim is only believable if the crew can survive a day that is not an emergency.
- **Batch climax:** Chapter 20. A stair pressure turn exposes a scored bronze delivery seat, and the group must choose between a safe limited continuation, a costly retreat toward the flooding Cinder Steps, or an admission claim that would violate Hearthhold consent. They choose limited continuation under named stops and a named review.
- **Required character changes:** Marek's control flaw becomes a willingness to leave blanks on a page. Jo acquires a real, priced maintenance role. Pell and Leena each force a work practice to change. Iven's guild cost becomes visible and reportable.
- **Required power changes:** None. No bearing mark, named stage, repeatable reading, route change, binding, relay, or automatic repair. The four load impressions in this batch are explicitly unusable.
- **Required world reveals:** A Hearthhold is inhabited and self-maintained; air authority and civic authority are separate; a maintenance charter cannot be written by the person whose work it describes; an unnamed service is not a free labor supply.
- **Open questions after the batch:** Who replaces the scored delivery seat, who pays or rotates Jo's deferred tool-route work, what the returning stair's missing receiver was for, what the empty-seat plate means, and what Marrowgate does with a still-unsigned Form 7-B.

## Chapter 0011 — The Room That Holds the Terms

- **POV:** Marek.
- **Location/time:** Hearthhold air-gate anteroom, early afternoon, immediately after the Chapter 10 test.
- **Immediate goal:** Learn the actual admission procedure without pretending that a successful test equals a welcome, and get Leena a dry place to recover.
- **Resistance:** Sefu's authority is narrow and cannot speak for residents; Nera refuses to convert air approval into permission; the four residents each have their own reason not to be folded into one consent; the courier tries to make continuing maintenance look like guild custody.
- **Major turn:** Dalla Sen announces that the test observers are finished and that nobody agreed to keep a shift. Marek admits he asked Sefu about the test and never asked what came after it.
- **Emotional or practical consequence:** The four residents draw a line between having volunteered for a test and agreeing to maintenance. Sefu, Nera, and Iven each state the outer limit of their own authority in the same room.
- **Required continuity facts:** ten occupants for the fresh working interval, one of them on a litter; Renn on the landing and Mikka at the sill, both uncounted until a named return is recorded; the courier beyond the incline; the route record shows the six as present for attended maintenance with no civic admission and no claim.
- **Ending hook:** The residents ask for a named maintenance shift and a correctable route record before any deeper admission is discussed.
- **Delivered:** Chapter 11.

## Chapter 0012 — The Work That Keeps Water Down

- **POV:** Marek.
- **Location/time:** Anteroom, first resident-maintained pump shift, later the same day.
- **Immediate goal:** A safe maintenance rotation, not a permanent pump.
- **Resistance:** Sediment under the bronze seat, a chattering check, a pinched housing, a crooked retaining bolt, the temptation to leave the bulk tools below unrecorded, and Marek's own wrong plan to jack the frame.
- **Major turn:** Jo Verrick refuses to let the frame be raised on a packed foot and explains why a jack would turn a defect into a hazard. Nera stops Marek from closing the outer service door while the well is open, because that door is the relief draft and the escape route.
- **Emotional or practical consequence:** Marek revises the plan in public and the room accepts the correction without anyone losing standing. Iven teaches the work rather than keeping it.
- **Required continuity facts:** the gland stays installed; the bronze seat is not scored; the pump draws flooded sump water; Oren and Tams carry the frame; Pell calls readings from the bench.
- **Ending hook:** The first shift closes with a named cost: Jo's half-day of deferred tool-route work, written as a cost and not as community labor.
- **Delivered:** Chapter 12.

## Chapter 0013 — A Charter Is Not a Signature

- **POV:** Marek.
- **Location/time:** Anteroom, late afternoon.
- **Immediate goal:** Turn the route record into one clean agreement.
- **Resistance:** The old inspection charter covers the incline and neither the service shaft nor the Hearthhold gate. Residents need consent, withdrawal, and maintenance language. Marek's instinct is to fill the blanks; Dalla's answer is that the blanks are where nobody has agreed.
- **Major turn:** The disagreement over whether the crew's labor makes them beneficiaries, claimants, or temporary workers. Jo refuses the first two categories outright; Mikka refuses to let meals make the crew beneficiaries.
- **Emotional or practical consequence:** Withdrawal is defined: it stops new admission and new work while the escape route stays open until the room empties. Iven states that translating the repair does not transfer the old craft out of the room that taught it.
- **Required continuity facts:** the guild line stays closed by choice; the retreat route is recorded as physically continuous and not certified.
- **Ending hook:** A useful maintenance draft that is deliberately not a charter.
- **Delivered:** Chapter 13.

## Chapter 0014 — The Hearthhold Beyond the Gate

- **POV:** Marek.
- **Location/time:** Common kitchen, clinic and school corner, and boundary workbench beyond the gate; the next morning.
- **Immediate goal:** Learn what the Hearthholds actually call a safe room in daily practice, without touring or claiming a neighborhood.
- **Resistance:** Mikka's limit that six outsiders do not become six residents; Sefu's two concrete exclusions; Jo closes the door to her sleeping room before anyone can look; the meal queue turns the room back into a kitchen; and Marek's habit of reading the room as a load diagram.
- **Major turn:** Leena tells Marek he is calculating who is allowed to be a person, and Mikka names a safe room as one where the pot does not block the clinic door. Jo asks for a carpenter who will explain the pin and not claim the bench.
- **Emotional or practical consequence:** Iven explains and helps cut a new air-baffle retaining pin while Jo keeps the measurement and tags the old one for the repair shelf.
- **Required continuity facts:** the crossing is one work period, by resident request; the clinic cot is care, not residence; the deeper gate is closed again by Sefu without asking the crew.
- **Ending hook:** The crew has a work area, not citizenship or ownership.
- **Delivered:** Chapter 14.

## Chapter 0015 — The Guild Line Is Closed

- **POV:** Marek.
- **Location/time:** Anteroom and inspection incline, the day after the work period.
- **Immediate goal:** Bring the guild order into ordinary consequences without turning it into a fight.
- **Resistance:** Iven's suspended-bond receipt makes his maintenance work reportable; Nera and Sefu must keep their separate roles; the locked door turns a delay into a constraint.
- **Major turn:** The courier states that Form 7-B is still unsigned in Marrowgate and that the record names who answers for the collapse, which is the crew's first reminder that a surface liability is still open.
- **Emotional or practical consequence:** Iven explains the bounded work to Dalla in plain language and accepts that it may be reported. The crew inventories local stock and records the absent seal, the single drive-dog key, and the locked guild pressure dial.
- **Required continuity facts:** the guild cannot reach the pump; it can still refuse parts, records, and rescue; the order freezes the surface door, route lamp, upper-catch chain, and guild tool case.
- **Ending hook:** A decision to use local stock until it is safe to continue, and a costly retreat toward the Cinder Steps recorded as a real option rather than a promise.
- **Delivered:** Chapter 15.

## Chapter 0016 — The Workday at the Boundary

- **POV:** Marek.
- **Location/time:** Anteroom, a full day with no crisis.
- **Immediate goal:** Learn what maintenance looks like when nothing is currently breaking.
- **Resistance:** Fatigue, conflicting schedules, and the residents' need to keep their own routines. Jo's own shutter route goes long and she is not back when the pump interval is due, so the interval closes short with the water below target.
- **Major turn:** Marek asks to record the real reason rather than a flattering one. Separately, Pell refuses a smaller task chosen for him and is given Jo's tool-sorting and mark-reading instead.
- **Emotional or practical consequence:** A daily work board appears with seven tasks, a name for each, and a rule that any name comes off when the work changes. Leena corrects the sheet rather than remaining only a patient.
- **Required continuity facts:** five liters from the collapse plus the sealed condensation liter; during named work periods the kitchen's provision is the crew's drinking supply and the sealed liter stays sealed.
- **Ending hook:** A work practice changes before the route does, and no one calls it citizenship.
- **Delivered:** Chapter 16.

## Chapter 0017 — When the Air Path Changes

- **POV:** Marek.
- **Location/time:** Anteroom, clinic corner, and upper landing during a stair pressure turn.
- **Immediate goal:** Keep residents and the injured crew safe while preserving the conditional route.
- **Resistance:** A wet mineral seam beside the pump housing, the returning-stair pressure cycle heard through a route that seemed to bypass it, a fevered clinic patient who cannot be evacuated, and a tempting service-hatch shortcut that Nera and Marek both reject.
- **Major turn:** Nera and Marek refuse the bypass for different physical reasons, and the anteroom is cleared to the upper landing by the air condition Nera and Sefu set in the scene. That condition is not yet written into the work sheet; the sheet that records it is drafted in Chapter 19.
- **Consequence of the turn:** Dalla separates the five standing watches a pressure turn requires — air valve, civic wheel, upper landing, clinic return, and the work-bell relay — from the three standing obligations attached to the old return route itself, and holds that an unfilled watch stops the shift rather than shortening it. Five new watch obligations are created, none of which any room test had named.
- **Required continuity facts:** the pump chamber stays isolated; the bypass hatch stays latched; the anteroom is unusable during a pressure turn; the clinic return stays watched.
- **Ending hook:** The route reopens under conditions that have just been revised, and the pump is limited to short attended intervals.
- **Delivered:** Chapter 17.

## Chapter 0018 — The Stair in Someone's House

- **POV:** Marek.
- **Location/time:** The returning stair's fixed first landing, on an attended trip.
- **Immediate goal:** Decide whether to map the stair for a future route, warn the Hearthholds, or leave it closed.
- **Resistance:** The mechanism is a damaged function with a visible cycle and a missing receiver; Renn's school-and-infirmary history conflicts with the guild record without answering anything; Pell's splinted ankle and Leena's sling make a test crossing unjustifiable.
- **Major turn:** Marek names all three options aloud and chooses them in order: map it, tell the rooms, close it. Sefu enforces the closure.
- **Emotional or practical consequence:** A black cord and a Hearthhold mark record the restriction; Renn's local history is entered as testimony rather than a survey correction.
- **Required continuity facts:** walking human load repeats the return, a dead pack does not; the guild may call it a faultform while local use shows a function that failed; the lower side cannot be certified.
- **Ending hook:** The map now points to work required before anyone can call the stair a route.
- **Delivered:** Chapter 18.

## Chapter 0019 — The Work Sheet Other Hands Could Read

- **POV:** Marek.
- **Location/time:** Anteroom, the day after the stair restriction.
- **Immediate goal:** Make the maintenance work legible to the people who will inherit it.
- **Resistance:** Disagreement over who pays, who can refuse, and what happens if the pump fails while the crew is asleep. Marek's hand cannot keep a line, and a dictated record is not the same as one made by its users. Every condition has to survive an objection, so Jo questions whether "water at the sill" is an air rule or a pump rule, and Nera rewrites it so nobody must prove they were right before moving.
- **Major turn:** Iven talks the pump sequence aloud at the grate so Dalla can write it in her own hand, which is how the craft stops being his private property. Dalla becomes the recorder of results.
- **Emotional or practical consequence:** The sheet states that no pump runs overnight without a named watch, and that withdrawal stops new work and admission without trapping anyone. Leena's care visit to the clinic is staged on the page as care. Jo refuses to let the sheet be marked as agreed while the delivery seat is still unopened, so the batch's signature event is reserved for Chapter 20.
- **Required continuity facts:** Nera writes the air limits, Sefu writes consent and stop conditions, Jo's cost stays a cost, and the sheet grants no ownership and creates no public route.
- **Ending hook:** A legible draft that nobody has agreed to, with the review line drafted and unmarked.
- **Delivered:** Chapter 19.

## Chapter 0020 — A Route That Must Be Kept

- **POV:** Marek.
- **Location/time:** Anteroom, the review called on the sheet's own change clause.
- **Immediate goal:** Keep the conditional route through an ordinary pressure and maintenance change without violating consent.
- **Resistance:** The delivery seat is scored and has taken a set, no spare bronze seat has been released, the guild line offers no part or rescue, the stair remains restricted, and retreat through the service bypass would mean carrying the same injuries over worse ground. The sheet is also argued rather than filed: Nera forces a written condition for an unfilled watch, and Jo catches the word *route* still serving as the sentence's subject after Marek crossed it out of the heading in Chapter 19.
- **Major turn:** Marek proposes deeper admission to bring the litter inside, Mikka names it an admission request rather than a maintenance decision, Sefu refuses it, and Marek records the request as refused instead of pushing. Nobody signs a continuation over a seat nobody can promise.
- **Emotional or practical consequence:** Sefu reads the sheet aloud before any mark is made, and Dalla records the reading time. This is the batch's only signature event.
- **Required continuity facts:** the scored seat and the new limit are written beside the old one; short attended intervals only; no overnight run; a replacement or a safe local substitute required at review.
- **Ending hook:** A limited continuation with a named cost, a named review at the second work-bell after the next complete pressure turn, and a route six outsiders do not own.
- **Delivered:** Chapter 20.
