# Novel Specification

Title: The Dungeon Architect
Repository slug: novel-dungeon-architect
Genre: progression fantasy / engineering
Male lead: A civil engineer who designs safer paths through a living dungeon
Length target: 800 chapters
Relationship policy: One slow-burn relationship or no romance, decided in the bible phase.
Power rule: Growth must be earned through practice, cost, failure, relationships, and changed decisions. The lead must not become instantly overpowered unless the approved genre premise requires it.

## Premise
A civil engineer trapped in a dungeon discovers that every room is a structural problem, not merely a monster encounter.

## Ending
The dungeon becomes a self-governing city rather than a new empire owned by its builder.

Volume 02 Batch 0001 **used** that and did not reopen it: the boundary named a work, the timber came up out of a flooded gallery on a fourth air document, a second shore went under the lip, and the post is still not built. **Talla Roke arrived with a lawful written order and withdrew it in her own hand at a cost to four households; a Cartwright clerk's page now exists and the guild's claim is unresolved; a door was heard eleven feet down and nobody has been there; and a schoolteacher asked out loud *who keeps the Hearthhold rooms*, and the room did not answer.** The volume's question is open, sharper, and more expensive: why do the oldest plans in this building show homes and not treasure, and can a route built by a rescue crew and kept by the people who sleep under it become a public thing without becoming a road with a name, a page in a file, and a lock on the end?
