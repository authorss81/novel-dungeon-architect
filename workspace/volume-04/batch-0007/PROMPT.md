## What is new in this batch

## Craft — the four rules are now law, and they are not optional

Batch 0006 was the second batch written under them and its own measured baseline is in `reviews/volume-04/batch-0006.md` with a method beside every figure. **Do not inherit a figure from that file that you cannot reproduce on the delivered files; a craft figure is a measurement or it is not a figure.**

1. **No epigraph block and no closing recap block. The only bold in a chapter is a person speaking.** Batch 0006 delivered **zero** fully-bold paragraphs. **Write the room, and do not write the morning's findings after it.** Each of its seven chapters opened on the room and closed on the room, and the sixth-bell figures sat in four different shapes. Do the same in four shapes across five chapters.
2. **The request-to-speak tag is dead as a device. The half against is canon and must be in your chapters, and asking for it in the same four words is retired.** Batch 0006 finished with **zero** demand-for-speech openers in seven chapters, measured as a paragraph opening with a bare imperative of speech followed by a second word, after ten were drafted and all ten re-shaped. **A room asks in whatever shape the speaker has, and several answers arrive with nobody having asked.** The clerk's recitations are carried in plain narration by default, and the ones that stay speech are the ones that are acts. **The half against is a canon beat and must be in every chapter, in different mouths, and must not open a speech in the same four words twice in the same chapter.**
3. **Quoted share is a real constraint. Every word inside matched quotation marks over total words, per file, never across files. Batch 0006 ran 48.1 to 59.6 with a weighted mean of 55.3.** Land between roughly forty-five and sixty-five; a chapter above seventy is a chapter with no scene left in it. **Narration must carry the room, the objects, the weather of a working morning and the interior life.**
4. **Pressure must come from a decision, not from a delivery.** A document may arrive; it may not be the cause. Batch 0006's three escalation chapters were three different kinds — character conflict, a decision with a cost, a resolution — and **this batch is five chapters and the volume's climax, so at least three of the five must be different pressure types, and the two that are hardest to tell apart must be named before you write them.** **And name the bind before you write the decision. A decision whose cost nobody has stated in a room is a decision the room agreed to rather than made.** Batch 0006's own lesson: the run-up chapters were money and a fact from a counter, and the escalation was conflict, a cost, and an arrangement, and none of the five was set running by an arrival.

**One craft measure Batch 0006 failed to report and you must report: the tag *said*.** Batch 0006 counted it at 28, 22, 38, 45, 56, 40, 50 and converted roughly forty attributions into a small physical beat, a location or a different verb, and **the remaining ones are short interjections where a bare tag is correct English and no count will improve them.** Do the same and count them. A later phase that wants the tic out should convert attributions, not count words.

**And one number that is not a target: the 4000-word ceiling is retired.** It is not in `AGENTS.md`, `NOVEL_SPEC.md`, `outline/volume-04.md` or the bible, and Batch 0006 delivered 3357, 2690, 4079, 4424, 4589, 3072 and 4131 with three of seven over it. **Report lengths as measurements and do not trim delivered prose to hit a figure that was never canon.**
