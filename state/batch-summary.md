# Batch Summary — Volume 01, Batch 0001

## Outcome

Chapters 1–10 are complete and reviewed. Marek's six-person Public Works party survives a real Marrowgate tunnel collapse, reaches the first Lattice rooms, fails a six-person refuge at full-room test, finds domestic evidence of an old civic refuge, studies a physically continuous returning stair, and opens a short attended route to a Hearthhold air-gate anteroom. The batch ends with a conditional local victory, not a crossing into Hearthhold Middle and not a permanent escape.

## Movement

- Chapters 1–2 establish the collapse, six-person roster, Leena's right-shoulder injury, the eight-liter recovery, and a six-person/two-hour lean-to.
- Chapters 3–4 separate the Cartwright's old clearance claim from physical safety, reveal uphill water, identify the failed reciprocating sump pump, and use a temporary flood-gate delay.
- Chapter 5 runs the five Room Checks at the declared six-person load; bearing, breath, drainage, escape, and change all fail. Marek's controlled breach costs his right-hand fine control and the crew's bulk supplies.
- Chapters 6–8 establish domestic use, a verified one-liter condensation reserve, the wrapped empty-seat plate, Sefu's first boundary signal, the physical returning-stair cycle, and Pell's left-ankle sprain.
- Chapter 9 introduces Nera and Sefu as separate air and civic authorities, rejects Marek's recorded air plan, installs and tests the split gland seal and drive-dog key, then ends with the batch's first brief Nera viewpoint shift. Renn Vale, Dalla Sen, Jo Verrick, and Mikka Hale volunteer for the next room test. Chapter 10 confronts distinct Cartwright legal instruments, lets Iven sign the maintenance sheet rather than Dael's custody order, and establishes the anteroom's conditional twelve-occupant test in scene: eleven adults, fourteen-year-old Pell, one stretcher, and 120 kilograms of gear for thirty minutes. Only after the pass does the room receive its two-hour working rating of ten people including one stretcher, subject to named stop and evacuation conditions.

## Canon and resource handoff

- Water ledger: 24 liters at shift start; 8 recovered; 6 after Chapter 2; 5 from the collapse after Chapter 3; one additional liter of verified condensation reserve sealed in Chapter 6. The sump is pumped, not consumed.
- The failed pump is repaired mechanically: a split bronze-and-fiber gland seal and missing drive-dog key are installed; twenty no-load strokes, a five-minute draw-down, a fifteen-centimeter sump drop, a dry gland, unchanged air cloth, and a dry flood gate are witnessed. The delivery check chatters, so attendance is mandatory.
- Marek has no formal power stage, bearing mark, or repeatable reading. His right hand has permanent fine-control loss; Leena's shoulder is reduced and protected; Pell's left ankle is splinted. Oren and Tams remain alive.
- The returning stair has a visible movable slab, throat, rope, and counterweight. Its missing lower receiver causes a visible load cycle; it is not teleportation and is not resolved.
- The wrapped empty-seat plate remains unexplained and is laid on a work cloth on the anteroom bench in Chapter 10 rather than appearing there without on-page handling.
- Nera controls air testing and mechanism operation. Sefu independently controls civic admission. The old clearance seal, route charter, salvage lien, insurance notice, route-custody order, physical ownership, and non-claim receipt remain distinct.

## Relationship and thematic change

Marek's refusal to sign Form 7-B cannot prevent the collapse, but he accepts Leena's injury and names his uncertainty. Leena becomes the person who forces stated limits. Iven moves from guild caution to a costly maintenance signature. Nera's first meeting is professional friction and earned respect, not rescue or romance; her brief Chapter 9 interiority emphasizes that technical success does not cancel civic refusal. Pell's refusal to hide his ankle and Sefu's insistence on a veto turn survival into a question of who may refuse and who must maintain the route.

## Open handoff

The Hearthholds must decide whether to admit the six beyond the conditional anteroom test; a maintenance charter must be written with residents rather than claimed by the first builder; Dael's order and the locked guild line will contest the route; the pump needs a real maintenance plan; the returning stair and empty-seat plate remain unresolved; and the wider Lattice mystery remains open. The Volume 01 Chapter 50 resolution remains future work.
