The surface incline is physically closed; the courier names unsigned Form 7-B as an open record; Iven's witness bond is suspended; the crew stops counting on guild rescue, parts, or records. - Chapter 17 uses the wet mineral seam, pump housing, clinic return, and returning-stair pressure cycle to force an air reversal. The bypass shortcut fails. The revised route requires five standing watches during a pressure turn — air valve, civic wheel, upper landing, clinic return, and the work-bell relay at the threshold — plus evacuation to the upper landing on any watcher's call, and an unfilled watch stops the shift rather than shortening it.

Renn's school/infirmary history conflicts with the guild record without solving the missing receiver. The stair is black-cord restricted and its condition recorded for later work.
- Chapter 19 makes the maintenance work legible without making it agreed. Iven talks the pump sequence aloud so Dalla can write it in her own hand; Nera's air conditions and Sefu's civic conditions are argued line by line, each by a named person objecting to a specific wording. Jo refuses to let anyone mark the sheet as agreed while the delivery seat is still unopened, and the review line is drafted and left unmarked.
- Chapter 20 is the local climax and the batch's only signature event. A pressure turn exposes a scored delivery seat and a check that has taken a set. The crew refuses forced deeper admission, weighs a costly retreat toward the flooding Cinder Steps, and chooses limited continuation through short attended intervals. Nera forces a written condition for an unfilled watch, and Jo catches the word *route* still serving as the subject of Marek's decision sentence after he crossed it out of the heading in Chapter 19, with Dalla copying the correction so the two records cannot drift. Sefu reads the sheet aloud, the reading time is recorded, and only then are the marks made. The pump, air gate, stair restriction, and guild order all affect the decision.

## Water and material handoff

During a named work period the Hearthhold kitchen's provision is the crew's drinking supply, and the sealed liter is opened only if a shift overruns or a person is unwell. Hearthhold meals and small recovery cups are counted separately. The pump draws flooded sump water, not drinking water. The kitchen provision is an unpaid obligation the crew has not settled.
- The pump retains the split bronze-and-fiber gland seal and restored drive-dog key. Its delivery check still chatters under sediment, and the bronze delivery seat is now scored. The pump is limited to short attended intervals; no spare seat has been released; no overnight running is permitted.
- Iven's old flat file has a cord-bound grip and teeth worn to a shallow row: it will not take a heavy cut but will draw a burr off a soft face, which is why it is the finishing tool only. A second, smaller file is the light work. Both live in Jo's basket and carry no ownership claim.
