# Chapter 143 — The Third Asking

**Open for the corridor on the third day of the twenty-eighth week, a Wednesday, and the school ran for the nineteenth morning in the room at the kitchen-line end, and a clerk asked five people for four hours of a piece of work in a room with the terms out loud, and named the three she was not going to ask and the reason for each before she asked anybody anything, and the reasons were not the reasons she gave last month.**

**Four hours, asked for in a room with the terms out loud: the crew's column moved by four, from fifty-nine and a half to sixty-three and a half, and two pairs of hands walked four rooms and found that three of the four second ways out of this network are rooms and the fourth one is a word.**

At the fourth bell the nine went in and one of them went in sitting down, and the strip of crate board under the ninth hearth stone was holding and the two flags had not moved, and Jo Verrick said the standing term out loud in the doorway the way she has said it every morning since a physician gave it to her, and Mero Senn said it out of his own hand against hers, and Ilsa Norr said yes to it, and Dalla Sen entered that the term is the same in two hands and did not use the word *read* about it, because the word is in a book four days up a road and a clerk does not use a word for a thing she has not done.

Then the clerk had the anteroom at the second bell and said what she was doing before she did it.

"**The crew have been asked twice in twenty-eight weeks. Both times it was a work and both times the terms were said out loud in a room and a person was named who could be told no, and I am asking a third time and I am going to name the three I am not going to ask and the reason for each before I ask anybody anything, because that is the practice and because the reasons have changed since the second time and I am not going to use the old ones in a new month.**"

"**Leena Orr. Her right shoulder is in a sling and takes no weight and that has not changed and I am not going to pretend it has. The reason she is not being asked is a new one and it is Renn's and I am putting it in Renn's words, because it is about a question Renn is going to have to ask somebody in a room in the next two days and it will be asked in this building and I do not want the woman who answers it to have spent the morning carrying a lamp.**"

"**Pell Roke. He is fourteen. The reason is not his age and I want that in the book, because the age is the reason everybody expects. The reason is that he reads numbers, and this work is not a number, and the end of this work somebody is going to stand in a room and be asked how many people were in it, and he will be the one holding the chalk in this building, because he has been the one holding the chalk in this building since the twentieth week. And a count taken by the man who reads numbers is a count that nobody can argue with, and this building has spent four weeks being frightened of exactly that.**"

"**Tams Quill. His right hand is open and flat and is not a working hand and that has not changed either and I am not going to pretend it has. The reason he is not being asked is that he is the person who reads the board, and the board is the place in this building where a thing that has been done gets entered, and if he is also one of the two pairs of hands that goes into those four rooms then the same man is the hands and the witness and the record, and there is nobody left in this Hearthhold who can stand in a room and say that the record is not the man.**"

Nobody argued with the three reasons and nobody improved them, and Dalla Sen entered each of them as it was given, and then she asked the two.

"**Iven Tallo. Four hours, and I am naming the split before you do, because you always do and I would rather it was in my hand than yours. Two hours of yours and two hours of Marek Venn's. You go first because you are a carpenter and there is nothing in this work that a carpenter does better than anybody else and I would like that said.**"

"**And the terms, and they are out loud and Renn is the person who can be told no and she is standing on the landing and she has heard them. Four hours. Four rooms: the kitchen, the room at the kitchen-line end, the common landing, the anteroom. In each room you say out loud, in that room, what that room's second way out is, and who keeps it, and the person who keeps that room has to agree with you or say why not. You write nothing. You bring nothing in. You take no wedge and no bar and you do not go near the waist's third one, which is up a quarter of an inch and a bit and is not to be touched.**"

"**And the last of the terms, and it is the important one. You are not to open anything. You are not to move anything. You are going into four rooms to find out what those four rooms have, and if one of them has nothing then what you have found is that it has nothing, and you come up the steps and say so and nobody has been improved out of a room by you.**"

Iven Tallo said yes to the two hours and said the thing he wanted entered, and it was not about the split.

"**Say the last of the terms back,**" he said. "**All of it. And then I want to say one word and I want the word struck out of the plan, because somebody is going to say it about these four rooms inside a week and I would rather it was struck now by me than argued about later by a man in a bad month.**"

"**The word is *brace*. And it is wrong. A brace is a support joined to a mark. I am a carpenter and I have been one for nineteen years and there is not one mark in this building that is not off the wall, and a thing with no mark in it is not a brace, it is four rooms and a floor, and if anybody calls it a brace then a man in a bad month is allowed to call it a temporary thing, and there is no temporary in this Hearthhold. There is a room with a propped stone in it and a corridor with water on a fill and a frame that is a finger out of true and none of those are going to be better in the morning.**"

Bel Ordry wrote *not a brace* on the back of a day-board, struck it once through, and read it out, and Dalla Sen entered it as a carpenter's own word against a word and said that she had entered it in the order it was said and that the order was the finding.

---

**Two pairs of hands went into four rooms for four hours and found out what four rooms have, and three of the four rooms turned out to have a room.**

They came up the nine steps at the fourth bell and Iven Tallo said the results in the anteroom in the order he had walked them, and nobody wrote them down, and Dalla Sen wrote them down anyway because she is the clerk and said so before she did it.

"**The kitchen. Its second way out is the room at the kitchen-line end, and I have got a reason for that which is that the corridor is shut three days in seven and the kitchen is not, and on the three days the corridor is shut the room at the kitchen-line end is the only place a person in the kitchen can go that is not a shut corridor.**"

"**The room at the kitchen-line end. Its second way out is a word. It has been a word since the twentieth week and it is said out loud in a corridor by the nine people who are going to be in it, one at a time, and the clerk repeats it, and nine days ago a girl of nine asked what the word was for and was told *in case*.**"

"**The common landing. Its second way out is the anteroom, and the anteroom's second way out is a word, and the stair on that landing is not a route and is not in the charter and there is a person under a slab over eleven feet of shaft and the post under that has never been built.**"

"**And the anteroom. Ten including one litter for no more than two hours, the outer service door, the pump attended, and the air engineer or the elected keeper able to stop it. And the door is a finger and a bit out of true at the head and is packed with stone and clay and holding and not fixed, and the fifth of the five has been broken since the middle of last month.**"

Marek Venn said the finding and he said it as a man who has been wrong about the ends of things in this building before, and nobody in the room let him soften it.

"**Four rooms. Every one of them has a second way out. Three of them are a room. And the fourth one is a word, and a word is not a way out, and this network has a second way out and it is the same word it was on the Friday in the twenty-fourth week, when eleven people said it in a corridor one at a time and it opened nothing.**"

"**I want that in a clerk's hand next to the four numbers the cook wrote on Monday and the word *waits* that Renn put in her own condition, because those three things are the whole of what I have learned in this building in four weeks and none of them is a design.**"

"**And I want the other half of it, which is that the three rooms that have a room are the best thing anybody in this Hearthhold has built since I came up this passage. A network whose second way out is a word is a network with a hole in it. A network with four rooms in it that can each be emptied into the next one is a thing that did not exist in this building this morning, and I did not design it. Four women and a man who keeps a page in his coat and a clerk with a piece of chalk designed it, out loud, in a room, and if I stood up here and told you I designed it I would be doing the thing I came here to stop doing.**"

"**The terms of the asking were read back out of the book against the original and compared,**" said Dalla Sen, "**and it is the same in two hands, and I am entering that the carpenter struck a word out of a plan's back before he went to do the work, and that the word was his, and that the plan says nothing at all about a brace.**"

"**And I entered the whole of it and I am entering now that the crew have been asked three times in twenty-eight weeks and that the three who could not be asked were named with the reason for each before the asking, all three times, and that the reasons are not the same reasons twice.**"

---

**At the sixth bell the board was walked, and the count was in a good mood for reasons he did not give, and the clerk read the six things and the day's number and the count and a number that has moved twice in twenty-eight weeks.**

The count stood at the board with the chalk in his left hand and his right one open and flat in his lap, and he read both columns, and named no day, and gave no reason for not naming one.

"**Four today and a hundred and forty-six since the keeper signed. The boundary is at two hundred and sixty-three and a quarter, and it adds by the day the work was done, and the two columns reconcile, and they have reconciled for twelve weeks.**"

"**And the crew are at sixty-three and a half. Up four. Fifty-nine and a half to sixty-three and a half, and it is the third time anybody in this building has asked the five of us for anything since the fifth day of the nineteenth week, and the first two times it was for work and this time it was for going into four rooms and saying out loud what is in them, and that is the first time anybody has asked us for a thing where the answer might be *nothing*.**"

"**Fifteen entries. Eleven nulls, one claim, one mark, one row and a plan. Nine in the ordinary column and six in the one with nothing over the top of it, and the column is not headed, and I am not going to give the ninth reason this month.**"

Dalla Sen read the fifteen entries out and read her own reading back and entered that the count had said *nothing* as though it were a job, and entered that she was not going to improve on it.

"**The pot is ten. The jars are two. The water is on the fill at the waist and it has not been off it and it is not moving, and there is still no number for that fill in this building.**"

"**The count. Wen Pask is seventy-eight days and seventy-eight nights into a count that is his own, and I am writing it, and I am entering that a bundle is still in this anteroom and that a man went into four rooms today and came back and said that three of them have a room in them and the fourth one has a word, and that nobody in this room joined those two facts, and that I did not want to.**"

---

**Two men came up the nine steps at the fourth bell with nothing in their hands, and the anteroom is ten including one litter for no more than two hours, and the fifth of its five terms has been broken since the middle of last month, and on Thursday a woman from a surface federation is going to ask to be in this room while a page is read out loud, and the page has thirteen rows on it, and one of the thirteen is a row nobody has ever agreed to.**

**Under two flags and a length of spent line, the channel at the low end of the corridor has silt in the bottom of it, and there is no grate in that frame and no second grate in this Hearthhold, and a page in a clerk's book that says somebody stands at the low end has been blank for twenty-eight weeks, and the third of the three conditions on it says that nobody is to be sent anywhere to make it true.**

**Nobody has been sent. Nobody has been asked. And on Thursday the room has to find out what it is going to do about a hole.**
