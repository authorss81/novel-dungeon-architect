## Immediate obligation for Volume 02, Batch 0001

- **OT-41 (new, and it is the batch's named next step):** **A post under the near landing's lip, and the timber for it.** The lip of the returning stair is one stone, carries the line-block at its base, is the edge of an eleven-foot shaft, and has **no post under it**. It went about two inches with a man under it and four people behind him, and the record says so. The post wants nine foot and a bundle. There is neither in this building. **The longest thing on hand is a length of five foot nine on the bottom shelf of the flooded lower pump gallery, with its inventory number chalked on it and the inventory line open**, and nobody is going down there this month: the gallery has four hours of standing air, there is one ladder, and a dead man's rope is tied round its stile. Recovering it is the same lawful question as Chapter 43's — a named work, Nera's inventory, Dalla's ledger, the keeper's receiving — and it is a harder question, because this time the air plan for that room exists and says *once*.
- **OT-42 (new):** **The four treads under the returning stair.** In Chapter 47 the woman from the wash rooms reported that behind the rail on the far side there are four treads going down, cut from the same dressed stone, dry, and that the lower receiving landing's floor is gone but the steps are not. Nobody has been down them. The far wall of the throat is eleven feet below and has never been seen. This is the volume's open physical question and it is also the first continuity back toward the Cinder Steps, and nobody in that boundary is going to walk it this month.
- **OT-43 (new):** **The route is a position and not a shield.** Iven's Chapter 38 warning has become a fact: the lower side of the stair is in service and there is a route in this Hearthhold with no charter, and the answer an office will want behind the question *who maintains the route* is a holder's name on a piece of paper. The crew's protection is that the route is maintained by the people whose rooms it is under, held by nobody, with a boundary keeper's name on a boundary's own line, and a slate that says **no person may add a name to it**. Iven said out loud that the wall will hold longer than people think and will cost somebody something that is not written down anywhere. **The guild has not yet heard about a shore.** Dael Harrow's office is forty days of walking away.
- **OT-44 (new):** **The closure clause, and the woman who wrote it.** Any of the four people who keep a room may shut the route; the shut carries a reason in the person shutting it's own hand; it is shut until somebody looks at the reason; and if it stays shut that is a decision with a name and a date read out at the next bell. Those eleven words are in the ledger **in her own hand and not Renn's**, because Renn already has everything. She also refused help on the crossing, in front of Renn, and that is on the record. She is the person in this building the crew was forbidden to ask and who has ended up holding more of the route than anybody except Renn.
- **OT-45 (new):** **The maintenance obligation is bounded and has an end condition that has not been met.** The crew hold the maintenance of what they built until the boundary can do it without them and a person named by the boundary has said so out loud, in the room, with the reason. That condition has not been tested. Iven's bond is still suspended, his instruction stands at twenty-nine uncompensated hours as last counted, and the Hearthholds will not pay him. Marek has said the six are going to leave and Sefu made him write the leaving as a **spring** conversation with a named air hand-over before he goes.
- **OT-46 (new):** **Nera's dead-window number and her refusal to publish it.** The turn is quicker now — about a second where it was a second and a half — and the ledger says only that the window is shorter and that the air engineer holds the number. **Nera's refusal to publish a rate is what the plan was built on and it is what Oren Sill died inside**, and nobody revoked it. Volume 02 inherits a boundary that runs a stair on a number nobody outside one woman has. She also has two readings on her own board she cannot account for, one of them the same shape as the Chapter 37 school-corner reading.
