## Blocking repairs applied

- **Chapter 11 occupancy count.** The chapter's whole argument is that the count is real, and Mikka was placing a cup beside a cot inside the rated anteroom while the record said she did not count toward the room. She now stops short of the sill because she has not been recorded as coming back in, Jo carries the cup in from the stone, and the route record names Renn at the landing and Mikka at the sill. No count, limit, or rating changed.
- **Flat file condition.** The file was cord-bound with unserviceable teeth in Chapter 12, shaping a pin head in Chapter 14, and given a new handle in Chapter 16. It now has teeth worn to a shallow row: no heavy cut, but enough to draw a burr off a soft face. Chapter 14 has Iven draw the head down a few strokes at a time and say why. Chapter 16 has him whip a fresh cord binding and add a second, smaller file for finishing, which also gives Chapter 16's existing "the small file is in the wrong place" beat a named antecedent.
- **Duplicate climax across Chapters 19 and 20.** Chapter 19 had already run the full signer list, the not-a-charter statement, and the next-review line that Chapter 20 repeats. Chapter 19 now ends on a draft nobody marks: the maintainers read their own sections back, Sefu and Nera sign only the conditions they wrote, and Jo refuses to let anyone agree until the delivery seat is opened. Chapter 20 keeps the single signature event — read aloud, reading time recorded, marks made — so the scored seat, not a second ritual, is the climax. The review line is now copied onto the landing sheet as the line drafted the day before.
- **Unestablished prop.** Chapter 18 put Pell on a bench at the stair landing; the only bench in the manuscript is the anteroom bench. He now sits with his back against the stone beside the fixed rail, crutch across his knees.
- **Watch count.** Chapter 20 wrote "the three pressure-turn watches" where Chapter 17 creates five. It now names all five — air valve, civic wheel, upper landing, clinic return, and the bell at the threshold — and adds that an empty watch is a stopped shift, not a shortened one. Chapter 17's separate list of three obligations on the return route is now explicitly distinguished from the five watches, and `state/continuity.md` records the same distinction.
