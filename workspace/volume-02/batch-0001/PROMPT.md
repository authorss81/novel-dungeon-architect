## What this batch's first job is

**It is the post under the lip, and it is the fifth foot nine that is under the water.**

The near landing's lip is one stone, carries the line-block at its base, is the edge of an eleven-foot shaft, and has no post under it. It went about two inches with a man under it. The post wants nine foot and a bundle. There is neither in this building, and **the longest thing on hand is a length of five foot nine on the bottom shelf of the flooded lower pump gallery, with its inventory number chalked on it and the inventory line still open.**

Retrieving it is the same lawful question as Chapter 43's and a harder one, because this time the air plan for that room exists and it says *once* on purpose. It therefore needs: a **named work** that somebody in the boundary has actually agreed to and not merely permitted; **Nera's inventory** covering it; **Dalla's ledger** recording the item, the time, and the receiver; and the **keeper's release** — and the receiver cannot be one of the five. The crew does not own any of it, has never owned any of it, and cannot take it back out of the shore without taking the shore out.

Make the second job **Talla Roke**, if the batch earns her. She is a courier, salvage picker, and route scout from the Hearthholds, twenty-two, and in the bible she is **Pell's older sister** — a fact that has still not been said in prose in fifty chapters. The honest occasion is not a coincidence: it is Chapter 12's standing condition, which says that a courier who wants the bulk tools moved must give **a written order saying where they go and who receives them**. A courier arrives with an order. That is a lawful, specific, already-recorded reason for Talla Roke to walk into this room, and the family recognition is then a fact about two people who were already in the same building rather than a reunion. If the batch cannot make the arrival beat, leave her out; do not force it.

The third job is the **guild**, and Iven's warning has become a fact: the lower side of that stair is in service and there is a route in this Hearthhold with no charter. He has already said in the record that the crew's answer is a position and not a shield, and that the wall will hold longer than people think and will cost somebody something that is not written down anywhere. Volume 02 is where that cost starts arriving. Do not resolve the claim in this batch.

The fourth job is the **volume's own question**, which Volume 01 deliberately left unanswered: *who keeps the Hearthhold rooms, and why do their old plans show homes rather than treasure vaults?* Something in this batch should make that question sharper and more expensive, not answer it. The old plans are the Cairn material that Iven already distinguishes from treasure; the school's register boxes in a locked box in the school store, under Ilsa Norr's key, are the oldest continuous record of this building about itself, and nobody has opened them for this.
