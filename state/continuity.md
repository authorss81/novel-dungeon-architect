## Power and relationship guardrails

Marek Venn is still at the ordinary-engineering/unschooled-eye level. **Nine written nulls, all written down, all giving nothing usable, six in the ordinary column and three in the column with no heading, and no power stage was reached or hinted at in this batch.** He is corrected **by himself, three times**, and once out loud by a woman on a litter: that the only part of the release that was his was agreeing with a sentence somebody else wrote; that the sixth minutes are a load in people he never priced; and that he would have put an answer inside the notice if he had been allowed to write it. **Leena Orr asked to have his silence in a room checked in a week, and he wrote it in a book of his own and showed it to nobody, and said that was the first thing he had done in that building in a week that he would want written down.** He asks out loud and is willing to be told no, and in Chapter 88 he walked as far as the turn and stopped, because a man who is asked to witness an asking is a different thing from a man standing in it.

**No viewpoint shift in the batch.** Chapter 58 was Batch 0001's single shift. **Batches 0002, 0003 and 0004 are three batches' work without one, and a fourth shift inside a thirty-chapter window would have to earn itself four times over.** Nera's cold, her refusal, her misreading of the plan and her four claimed costs are all carried in her own mouth and in what other people do about them. Recorded as an author decision.

**The no-bare-specification rule held.** Every record in Chapters 81–90 is staged as somebody reading it, somebody objecting to a specific line, and somebody else writing the correction in their own hand: the release's terms and the courier's refusal, the three column headings, the conditions entered one at a time, the guild notice read twice, the inventory's thirteenth line, the courier's question, the four sentences from the rail, the office's answer, the keeper's struck page, the fourth column, the nine refusals and Bel Ordry's four conditions, the three filled lines, the two columns of hours, and the last line read at the sixth bell. **There is not one specification block in the batch.** The one item an in-session reader marked as a list rather than a scene is the run of refusals in Chapter 90, and two of the eight have been converted into quoted exchanges with a reaction between them.

**The working practice held and produced the batch's work:** any change to a room people use is written by the person who will keep it. **Batch 0003 produced nine instruments in nine hands. Batch 0004 produced a plan, a ruling, a struck water-carrying, a corridor closure, a refusal to be quoted, a finding about the keeper's condition, a page that has left the building, and nine refusals and four terms — and exactly one object in the whole batch that a stranger can hold, and it is six sentences going back up the road, deliberately not a list, and it is the only document this boundary has ever sent that could be used against it.**

**Slow-burn relationship policy held for a fourth batch.** No intimacy, no recognition scene, no acceleration between Marek and Nera. **Their closest moment in this batch is not between them either**: it is Nera Oduya walking as far as the turn and stopping, because a man who is asked to witness an asking is a different thing from a man standing in it, and then standing on a landing while two people she has opinions about have a conversation she cannot hear the argument of.
