## Power and relationship guardrails

Marek Venn is still at the ordinary-engineering/unschooled-eye level. **Seven nulls, all written down, all giving nothing usable**, and in this batch a null he refused to write and a column with no heading that took the one instead. **No named stage, no bearing mark, no repeatable reading, no route change, no binding, no relay, and no automatic solution exists anywhere in the batch.** He is corrected less often than in Batch 0001 and is corrected once, by himself, in Chapter 64, when he admits he filled a box on a guild form in his head in four seconds and asks for the rule to go in the book.

**No viewpoint shift in the batch.** Chapter 58 was Batch 0001's single shift and it carried distinct interior knowledge that changed four decisions. **A second shift inside a two-batch window would have had to earn itself twice over and the batch did not need it: Nera's cold is established through her own mouth, through the cloth sinking, through Bel Ordry's ambiguity, and through her scheduling of the hand-over, and the reader gets her interior only as she says it aloud.** Recorded as an author decision.

**The no-bare-specification rule held.** Every card, slate, tally, page, form, return and margin in Chapters 61–70 is staged: the delivery-seat price is written line by line and read as it is written; the four air cards are not re-read; the tally is read with its items; the reply is read out of a case and the form is put flat on a bench; the hardwood inventory is thirteen lines taken apart by nine people over an hour and forty minutes, with four owners correcting their own entries; the failure of the named work is read out nine times because Jo Verrick objects to a word in it.

**Inherited Volume 01 defect, recorded and NOT repaired, and it should be repaired by a Volume 01 decision.** `chapters/volume-01/chapter-0045.md` says that Oren Sill's packing of the shore's foot *is the whole of what the crew is going to lose in the forty-eighth*. **"The forty-eighth" can only mean Chapter 48** — a chapter reference inside the fiction and, read as a date, a day number above the locked maximum of forty-six. Batch 0002 rests on Oren's method, quotes it once in full, and never repeats the phrase. The sentence itself was **not edited**, because Volume 02 may not rewrite Chapters 1–50.

**The working practice held and produced the batch's best work:** any change to a room people use is written by the person who will keep it. **Nera's hardwood inventory, Mikka's book of favours, Bel Ordry's refusal to be thanked, Ilsa Norr's correction of her own ownership to *the school, held by Ilsa Norr*, Renn Vale's reason for not putting the fault in a guild file, Renn's refusal to have the woman from the wash rooms' eleven words copied into a slate, the woman's own eleven words said again in a colder month, Jo Verrick's estimate moved by hand, and a clerk's correction to her own failure entry — nine new instruments, in nine hands, and not one of them a document anybody outside this building can hold.**

**Slow-burn relationship policy held.** No intimacy, no recognition scene, and no accelerated beat between Marek and Nera. Nera remains an independent air engineer and a civic adult. **Their closest moment in the batch is not a moment between them: it is a question Marek asks Jo Verrick in a corridor, at the end, about a post that has no name against it.**
