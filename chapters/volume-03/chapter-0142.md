# Chapter 142 — Each Room Into The Next

**Shut for the corridor on the second day of the twenty-eighth week, a Tuesday, and the school did not run, and a plan of twelve rooms was argued into four rooms and a floor between them in an anteroom, in public, out of the third column of a page nobody has signed, and a woman struck one room out of it in nine seconds without coming down the steps and gave no reason at all.**

**Every network has an end. This one's end is a door, and the reason it is a door is that the one place in this building that could have made it the whole of this building has no row on the page, and the refusal of that row stands, and nobody re-argued it.**

By the first bell the plan was on the anteroom table in four parts and nobody touched it, and Dalla Sen said what she was doing before she did it, which is that the third column is the only list of anybody in this Hearthhold that nobody has had to write this month.

"**I am going to read the third column out loud, one row at a time, and the person whose row it is is going to stand up, and that is the whole of the design and it takes an hour and I am not going to apologise for it.**"

"**A room is a room until somebody can put the next room's people into it. And a room that can take the next room's people is not a room any more, it is a link, and a link needs two things a room does not: a count of what it is holding, and a person who says so out loud while it is happening. That is all. There is no other thing in it and anybody who tells you there is wants something.**"

"**And a change to a room people use is written by the person who will keep it. So the four of you who are going to keep these four rooms are going to write your own conditions, and I am going to read them back against what is written, and the man on the bench is going to say whether the arithmetic holds.**"

**Mikka Hale went first, because the kitchen is the room that everybody in this Hearthhold comes through twice a day and she has been in this building twenty-eight weeks and does not waste a room.**

"**I do the kitchen line and I do the pot, and the pot is ten days and has not gone up since the fifth day of the nineteenth week, and I do not light the hearth in the corner of the schoolroom and I have been asked about that four times this week and I would like it asked a fifth time and refused a fifth time.**"

"**Write this as my condition and I will not argue with it: the kitchen is emptied into the room at the kitchen-line end, and the room at the kitchen-line end is emptied into the common landing, and there is no order in that, and the pot goes on while it happens. Not because the pot matters. Because a pot going cold in a building is a sign somebody has stopped, and a sign that somebody has stopped is the only clock this Hearthhold has that nobody has to wind.**"

"**And the number. I will put a number in the first column because Renn asked to see it and who wrote it, and I will say again the thing I said the last two times and I am not going to say it smaller. I have not counted anybody. Four numbers and four rooms and I have not been in three of them this week.**"

Renn Vale wrote the corridor's own terms and did not write a number, and said why in a room with a clerk's chalk moving.

"**A number in a condition is a number somebody can satisfy, and a condition you can satisfy on a schedule is a date with a number on it, and I have said that sentence in this room for twenty-eight weeks and I am not going to be the woman who puts a figure in one in the week she says it. The first column is a permission and the cook is allowed to write permissions. The second column is mine and it takes no figures.**"

"**What I will write is this. The waist is a room people use and I am the person who keeps it, and on the first, second and fourth days of a week it is shut, and on the third, fifth and sixth it is open four at a time for four minutes in the pump's window with a bearer at the near lip, and that is what I have been doing for twenty-eight weeks and I am not changing it today because somebody has drawn four boxes.**"

"**And if it is to be a network then somebody has to say out loud what happens to the fifth person when four are on the waist, and the answer is that the fifth person waits, and the answer to the sixth is that the sixth person waits, and I would like the word *waits* written in somebody's hand on that page, because a network with no word in it for the fifth person is a corridor with a plan painted on it.**"

Ilsa Norr came down one step and did not agree to her own row being read to her, and said so before it was read.

"**The number in my row is a ten and it is a permission and I did not write it and the cook did, and the room has held eleven. I have said in a room that I am not going to say what the number was and I am not going to say it now, and I would like that read into the book with the word *now* in it, because now means it has not stopped.**"

"**My condition is the one in the tenth row and I am not writing a new one until Thursday, and the reason is that a woman on a landing asked me a question yesterday that I have not answered and I am not going to answer it by writing something down instead of saying it to her face.**"

"**And the network is a good idea and I want to say the half of it that is against it in my own trade and not in anybody else's. Nine children get moved out of a room with a propped stone in it, and the room they go into is a room with nine hearth stones in it, and the room after that is a landing, and the room after that is the anteroom, and my nine are on the fourth step of that anteroom for two hours at a stretch and the fourth one of them cannot stand for two hours. That is not a network. That is four rooms and a child on a step.**"

Bel Ordry stood up for the common landing, which nobody expected, and Dalla Sen entered that he had not been asked to.

"**The common landing is a landing. It is not a room and I am not going to pretend it is one, and if the four of you want four rooms then one of them is a landing with a stair head on it and there is a boy on that stair head with a crutch and an ear against a stone.**"

"**I will keep the landing. What I will not do is let a page say that a landing may hold a number of people, because a landing is where the stair is, and the stair is not in the charter and not in the plan and not in the second sheet, and I am not going to be the man who gets a number onto it by putting a box round it.**"

"**So the common landing takes what the room at the kitchen-line end gives it and gives it to the anteroom, and it does not have a number in the first column, and the reason it does not have a number is in the fourth column's reason and not in mine.**"

---

**Three steps up, a woman struck a room out of the network in nine seconds, before anybody had read the fifth one out, and gave no reason, and nobody asked.**

Nera Oduya had come down as far as the second step, which she does, and she had the network's list of rooms on the anteroom table in front of her in a clerk's hand because Dalla Sen had written it out at the first bell as a working list and not as a page.

"**Ask me for the air in those four rooms and I will give you the air in those four rooms and I will give it to you in a quarter of an hour and it will not be the air I would give a room with a stove in it.**"

"**And I am not going to say the half of that which is against me unasked, because this boundary learned that in the twentieth week and it is the only free thing in it. The half against me is that the fourth room in a chain that ends at a door is the one that decides whether the chain is a network, and a network with a fixed end is a queue with a plan, and a queue is a thing with a person at the front of it, and I do not know who that is going to be and I have not been asked and I am not going to be.**"

"**One more professional thing and then I will go back up the steps, because the air at the low end of that corridor is mine and it has been mine for twenty-eight weeks and I am not going to be standing in a room being useful while it is happening.**"

"**The water comes up the channel before it comes up the corridor. That is a fact I have had for twenty-eight weeks and it is the reason there is a blank page in this clerk's book and it is the reason the anteroom has a word for its second way out. If the water ever comes up the channel into a room with anybody in it, the air in that room goes before the water does, and the air gate is the only thing in this Hearthhold that can put air back into a place, and I am the only person who may operate it.**"

"**I have never used it as that. I want that entered and I want it entered in those words, and I am telling this room I have never done it and not that I would not.**"

Nine seconds, from the second step, in her own hand: the wash rooms went out of the list of rooms on the anteroom table, with a strike through them and nothing in the space where a reason would go, and she was back on the second step before anybody had finished reading the list, and nobody asked her, and the clerk left the space empty and entered that she had left it empty on purpose.

---

**Then a courier asked the network a question and the network did not have an answer, and the answer she wanted was not a drawing.**

"**I am going to say the test out loud and then I am going to go and stand somewhere else, because the test is not mine and I do not want to be in the room while somebody looks for it.**"

"**My rule is that a route that cannot be used by an ordinary resident is not a public route. I have had that in your clerk's book in my own words since the third day of last month. And that rule has never once been about a room, and you have just made a very good set of rooms, and I want to know whether the thing you have made is a set of rooms or a road with four stops on it.**"

"**So here is the test and it is one morning of my time. Put me at the kitchen line with a bundle that is not mine, on a day this corridor is open, with nothing in my hand and no mark of yours and nothing of yours at all, and let me walk it. And if I can get from the kitchen line to that door as a person with a bundle and no permit and no order, then it is a network of rooms and I will take thirty-one of your people up my road in the sixth day of this week and say so to a woman at a counter who is competent and is doing her job.**"

"**And there is one condition on the test and it is the only one and I am not being coy. Nobody goes at the front and nobody goes at the back. A thing with a person at the front of it is a queue, and a queue is a road, and a road is what the answer to the market form already is.**"

"**If you put somebody at the front of your four rooms then what you have built is a lane, and a lane is a thing somebody can shut, and I have watched a lane get shut. I am not doing it for you. I am doing it because if you build a lane and call it a network then in three months a woman at a counter will be able to say the word *network* at me and I will have to go back to the gate with a form with two lines in it.**"

Marek Venn said the thing that everybody else in the room was thinking and said it as a man who had put a length of rope on a floor a week before and been told what a list is.

"**Then we do not put anybody at the front, and that means the last one out of the kitchen line decides when the kitchen line is empty, and the last one out of the common landing decides when the common landing is empty, and the two of those people are two people we have not met yet, because this network has never been used.**"

"**And I am going to say the thing nobody in this room wants said on a Tuesday morning, and then I am going to go and stand on the step.**"

"**Your network ends at a door. It has to. The anteroom takes ten including one litter for no more than two hours, and the fifth of its five terms has been broken since the middle of last month because the elected keeper is up a road and nobody in this building knows where he is, and the waist is a woman, and the waist will not take thirty-one people at once and she is right not to.**"

"**And the one place in this Hearthhold that could have made your four rooms into the whole of this Hearthhold is the low end of that corridor, and it has no row on that page, and a man asked for one in a room on the Monday of the twenty-seventh week and a woman refused it and a woman found the shape of the refusal and a physician gave the other half of it, and it is in the clerk's book in three hands, and the order they went in is the finding.**"

"**I am not re-arguing it. It stands. A row is a promise and a promise about a place where nobody can stand is a lie told in a column. And a refusal to put a row there means there is no plan for it and a place with no plan is a place nobody is watching, and a place nobody is watching is how a man of twenty-two came out of a channel.**"

"**So the network is four rooms and a door and it is not the whole of this building, and it never will be while that refusal stands, and every correct answer anybody in this room gives me about it is an answer with a hole in it shaped like a man who is dead.**"

For a while the anteroom was quiet, and the pump got four strokes in, and the woman who keeps the road said the part of it that was hers.

"**Then it is four rooms and a door,**" said Renn Vale, "**and I will take my waist. And when you use it, on the third day of this week, the low end of that corridor will be in the same building as thirty-one people crossing a fill that has moved three times, and I want the whole of you to hear me say that I have said yes to a thing and said the reason for the yes in the same breath, and that the reason is that this building shut a room with eleven people in it three weeks ago and I am not doing that again and I would rather carry a risk than a rule.**"

---

**At the sixth bell the clerk read the six things and the day's number and the count, and did not walk the board, and entered that she had not walked it and why.**

"**Two today and a hundred and forty-two since the keeper signed. The boundary is at two hundred and fifty-nine and a quarter, and it adds by the day the work was done, and the two columns reconcile, and they have reconciled for twelve weeks. The crew are at fifty-nine and a half and have not moved since the second day of the twenty-sixth week and I am going to say the reason in the book on the third day of this week and not before, because the terms have not been said out loud yet and a reason given before the terms is a favour.**"

"**The pot is ten. The jars are two. The water is on the fill at the waist and it has not been off it since the Thursday of the twenty-sixth week and it is not moving, and there is still no number for that fill in this building and there is not going to be one.**"

"**The count. Wen Pask is seventy-seven days and seventy-seven nights into a count that is his own.**"

"**And I did not walk the board today and I want the reason in my own hand. I did not walk it because a woman struck a room out of a list in nine seconds in front of eleven people and gave no reason, and I have been clerk for twenty-eight weeks and that is the first time in twenty-eight weeks that I have watched somebody refuse to explain themselves and then write a thing down in a book about it, and I did not have the arithmetic in me for a board.**"

---

**At the fourth bell on the third day of this week the four rooms of that network would be walked by two pairs of hands out of the five of the crew, and each of them would be asked out loud, in that room, what its second way out is and who keeps it, and the answer in three of the four would be a room, and the answer in the fourth would be a word.**

**At some point after that a woman with a bundle that was not hers would walk the same four rooms as an ordinary resident, because a courier's rule in a clerk's book says a route that cannot be used by an ordinary resident is not a public route, and thirty-one people of this Hearthhold have never once been an ordinary resident of a road.**

**Two flags and a length of spent line lay over a hole in the low end of that corridor, and a page in a clerk's book about that place had been blank for twenty-eight weeks with three conditions on it, and the third of them says that nobody is to be sent anywhere to make it true, and the room had just decided that nobody was going to be asked and nobody was going to be sent and the plan would have no row there, which left a network of four rooms and a door, and a hole under the floor at the lowest point in it, and no plan.**

**Which left, in a building with two hands and a half of nothing in the crew's column, a network of four rooms and a door, and a hole under the floor at the lowest point in it, and no plan.**
