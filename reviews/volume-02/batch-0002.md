## Blocking repairs applied — prose

- **B1. `chapter-0067.md` inverted the crossing schedule in the mouth of the character who fixes it.** Jo Verrick's closing summary read *Six days a week, two crossings*, which implies twelve crossings and puts crossings on days the school runs — forbidden on the same page and stated five times in the same chapter. The arrangement is **three days, six crossings a week, two crossings on a shut day, at the first bell and after the fifth**. Corrected.
- **B2. `chapter-0067.md` used a word the chapter had already struck.** Bel Ordry is thirty-five, and fifty lines earlier the chapter stages Dalla Sen striking the word *boy* off the ledger and Pell Roke correcting it to *fourteen* in front of the room, and Dalla reading the correction back. Twelve lines after that, Jo's summary called him **a boy on a bell**. Now **a man on a bell**. The chapter polices the word and then keeps it.
- **B3. Bel Ordry's mornings did not add up.** He said *fourth morning running* and named three days, while Nera's entry and the Chapter 65 review both said **four mornings, three nothings, one unclassifiable**. The 4/3/1 count is the load-bearing one — it is what makes a future *knock* unfalsifiable — and it propagates to five other places, so the fix was made in the mouth rather than in the count: he now names **Sunday, Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday**, which reconciles exactly, and his own next sentence is **on each of these four mornings** rather than *every morning this week*, which the added day falsified. No downstream reference moved.
- **B4. `chapter-0063.md` put a present-tense event in the wrong week.** Nera's cost — *my own refusal and my own method are standing on opposite sides of the same wall* — was dated to the **twelfth** week in a chapter that says the **eleventh** week five other times, in a batch that assigns Chapters 61–67 to the eleventh week, and on the morning after Chapter 62's own *it is the eleventh week*. One word.
- **B5. Chapter 65's boundary column did not reconcile, in a book whose engine is exact arithmetic.** The total moved 65 → 81.5, which requires +16.5, and the itemised list summed to 16.25 with the unvalued *Mikka's pot, again, twice* sitting in it and the stranger's day headed *Three* while itemising to 2.5. The crew's column reconciled exactly, so the asymmetry was visible. Repaired in the prose and not in the total's convenience: the stranger's day now reaches its stated **three** because Tams Quill enters **half a day of his own** — he was in that anteroom on the fourth day with a stranger's form on the bench and said no whole sentence about it, and he cannot classify the half and puts it in anyway — and **Mikka Hale's pot is struck out of the column as money and not a day**, with the reason said aloud and an offer to add it in the kitchen's own book instead. The list now runs **65 + 16.75 = 81.75**, and Tams says the figure out loud. Both the arithmetic and the character beat improved: a man who counts the half that is ours now counts himself inside the other column.

## Repairs applied — state files

- **S1. `state/character-state.md` carried a fabricated claim.** It stated that Iven Tallo says in Chapter 68 *the local repair of the waist is the thing that killed him, done on purpose*. **Neither *kill* nor *purpose* appears anywhere in that chapter**, and he argues the opposite — *I am against it. I am against locally* — and defines the local repair as *a person*. The entry now records what he says: that **the repair is a person**, and that the answer in a guild file in four years will be *nothing changed, and a man from above stood on it*, and that this is why he is against it. This was the most serious state error in the batch, because it put a murder in a dead man's name into the file the next writer reads.
- **S2. `state/current.md` garbled the lip.** It read *a finger and a half to a finger and a half-and-three-quarters*. The measurement is **a finger and a half to a finger and three-quarters**, in Chapter 70, in `continuity.md`, in `character-state.md` and in the batch summary. Corrected to the figure the prose gives.
- **S3. All four now record that **the page is still blank, the name is in one woman's mouth and one man's ears and nowhere else, and nobody has been asked.** The prompt also states that this is a *second* unnamed-name thread and is distinct from the name Nera Oduya said in a room about the air valve hand-over, and that neither may be fixed without the asking.
- **S4. `state/batch-summary.md` misreported two of its own measured figures**, in the one section the previous review told the next batch to trust over the recorded ones. *She …* paragraph openings were recorded as 22 and measure **19** (so 35 across the batch with *He …* at 16, not 38). - **S5. - **S6. - **Everything the reviewer confirmed as working** — the guild arriving in the first half, the escalation arriving through Batch 0001's own machinery, Mikka Hale's harder question asked and unanswered, Renn Vale turning a rota into a calendar, the woman from the wash rooms finding a yes that costs her nothing she had, Bel Ordry's refusal to be thanked, Iven Tallo's *both of those are the same sentence*, the ending that is not an ending, and the exactness of the staging rule.
