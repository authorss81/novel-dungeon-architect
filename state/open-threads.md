- **The board — EIGHTEEN, 9 + 9, WALKED ONCE IN THE BATCH, WITH A SIXTH REFUSAL AND A SEVENTH.** The sixth refusal has a sixth reason — **a seal is a thing that is on a thing, and the day a withdrawal of two seals goes on that board the board has a second line on it, and the first line would say what was withdrawn and the second would say who withdrew it** — and the seventh is refused in 200 unprompted, before the clerk has the chalk up, with the seventh reason explicitly withheld and nothing manufactured. **A repair pass after an independent review made this series single-valued and corrected the read itself: it is eleven nulls and seven that are not nulls, which is the eighteen, and the six named ones are a claim, a mark, a row, a plan, a condition, a store and a brace; the sixth is 198's and is counted in the clerk's entry in the same paragraph; 198's own line now carries no ordinal at all, because what Tams Quill says there is a forecast about whatever comes next and not a refusal that has happened; and in 199 the clerk enters that nobody put anything in front of him that morning and that a no with nothing in front of it is not a seventh of anything. The seventh is 200's. A later phase may not count a seventh anywhere else.** The unheaded column is unheaded throughout, Tams Quill gives no reason in any of the three chapters, **and the batch ordinal is not restated.**
- **The wall — SPREADER IN, SIXTH PLACE STILL EMPTY, AND THE LINE ALONG THE LIME IS IN TWO PLACES FOR THE FIRST TIME.** Five and a half shores, a spreader under the head of the sill, the sixth place empty and the iron four days up a passage, a brace of a school's own beech, a bad repair on one face only, a keeper's nine behind that, and a line in the join that is now in two, unmeasured and unjoined. **Jo Verrick refuses four things about it in a room and the clerk enters all four as four refusals and not as one, and she rules out the pump and the water unasked and says her ear and her thumb both stay out of that gap.** **The carpenter's four steps are counted twice and are in his own mouth, and how he counts them is his and it is a carpenter's answer and not a survey.**
- **Nera — BOTH CONDITIONS UNMET AND UNASKED IN ALL THREE CHAPTERS, HER THEORY AND HER TWELFTH-WEEK NAME AND THE VALVE AND THE SPRING UNTOUCHED, AND SHE IS NOT LEAVING.** Her word of the seventh day of the thirty-third week is not said a second time and is not replaced. **She says out loud in 200 that she has none of it and not a part of it and that this is the first time it has happened.** Her going was not raised in this batch.

- **Bel Ordry — HE IS THE MAN IN THIS BUILDING MOST LIKELY TO BE ABLE TO ANSWER AND SAYS SO, AND THE BRASS RULE IS AT THIRTY WEEKS SILENT AND THE PLATE AT THIRTY-NINE UNOPENED, AND HE HAS SAID OUT LOUD THAT A SENTENCE IN A ROOM HAS NOW TRAVELLED AND THAT HE HAS SPENT THIRTY-EIGHT WEEKS EXPLAINING WHY THEY DO NOT.**

- **Marek Venn — HE HAS SAID IN A ROOM THAT THE REASON HE WAS NOT GOING TO ANSWER WAS THE BEST POSSIBLE REASON AND THAT HE IS NOT SURE IT WAS THE TRUE ONE, AND HE HAS GIVEN A SECOND AND WORSE HALF THAT HE DID NOT SOFTEN.** The volume's argument arrives in him from behind for the last time: an office took a figure off a piece of paper, and the figure was of a man with a name and a description and a reason to write both down, and the man never agreed to be a figure, and the refusal he has spent a fortnight being proud of in rooms and this morning are the same instrument, and there was no blank page in that building at the counter because the second line was full. **He is not a post, is not on either network, and no standing, post, column or name has come out of it.**

- **Untouched by anybody, and not answered by anybody in this batch:** the answer to the timber message, which is still up the middle passage; the sheet with the chosen date on it four days up a passage; **the courier's sentence about the two people who can stop the watcher, which is not raised and not brought closer;** the two crews' two reasons for not trusting each other; the air valve and the spring; the shim's two-year return and the fact that nobody has agreed the term; the four households at a second gate, with nothing and no figure; **and the four rooms on the other side of the join, which nobody has been through and which nobody in this volume can say anything about.**
