# Review — Volume 03, Batch 0004 (Chapters 131–140)

**This is a self-review, run in the same session as the prose. It is not an independent review and it does not claim to be one.** `reviews/volume-02/batch-0003.md`, `batch-0004.md` and `batch-0005.md` do not exist for a closed canon volume, **an independent review of Volume 03 Batch 0003 is owed because the file that exists disclaims independence in its own first line, and an independent review of this batch is owed on the same grounds.** Batch 0003's record was corrected after an independent review found a class of defect the first pass had certified as clean; **the finding below about markup is that class, and the finding about a duplicated paragraph in a delivered file is the same class as the paragraph-index finding in Batch 0003's own record, and this record is written so that Batch 0005 does not repeat either.**

## Scope and method

All ten chapters were read as delivered prose and then measured by the method that reproduces the recorded figures for Batch 0002 and Batch 0003 exactly: paragraphs that do not begin with a quotation mark, taken in the raw text with the bold markers left in place so that a bolded narrative opener does not count; a paragraph whose first character is an asterisk counted separately as a section-divider opener; and a ninety-character prefix for duplication. Word counts are raw `wc -w`. **Markup was checked separately, file by file, for an even asterisk count, an even quotation count, a paragraph-level quotation balance, no duplicated paragraph, and no trailing run of three or more asterisks.**

**The method was validated against `chapters/volume-03/chapter-0121.md` through `chapter-0130.md` before it was used, and it returned Batch 0003's recorded figures exactly on both opener measures: excluding bolded paragraphs, *The* 5, *Nobody* 2, *And* 0, *She* 10, *He* 9, twenty-six together; including them, *The* 22, *Nobody* 2, *And* 20, *She* 10, *He* 13, sixty-seven together; and *read back* 4 and *read it back* 5, nine together.** The method is sound.

**One recorded figure did not reproduce and is not relied on.** Batch 0003's record states *150 paragraphs that do not begin with a quotation mark and do not begin with a bold marker*; the delivered files return 139 on that measure and this review does not explain the difference. The count that carries information is the opener count, and that reproduces exactly. **Batch 0005 is asked to use the opener count and to stop quoting the 150.**

## What the batch delivers against the brief

- **The week clock is exact and the arithmetic laid over it is exact.** 131–135 the twenty-sixth week and 136–140 the twenty-seventh, each chapter on the matching day of its week, the corridor shut in 131, 132, 134, 136, 137 and 139 and open in 133, 135, 138 and 140, the school on the four open days in the batch. **No day number is attached to any in-batch event and the day-number register's highest value is unchanged at the forty-sixth day.** The ten running figures give the day's increment and the total since the keeper signed, the day list is 3, 4, 2, 3, 3, 4, 3, 2, 4, 3 and sums to thirty-one, the base implied by the two columns is the constant one hundred and seventeen and a quarter, and the total is two hundred and fifty-four and a quarter at Chapter 140 against two hundred and twenty-three and a quarter at Chapter 130.
- **The escalation item the volume outline named is delivered in full.** The flood comes. **The water in the school corner's bed joint, the water between the hearth stones of the room at the kitchen-line end, and the water in the channel at the low end are one subject, and Jo Verrick says she can say they are the same warmth and cannot say they are the same joint, which is the honest form of the claim.**
- **Both Compact cases are good and one of them is right.** **Pettin Rahl is not a villain and is not softened: a permitted occupancy that has to be believed instead of counted is how a room fills, and he has a real collapse with a real figure and admits his own part in it.** Hollis Ashe is cooperative, bounded and correct, and the batch does not let the cooperative one win the argument.
- **The market route is the only way out and it goes through a rule.** **The rule is a form with two lines and a space in each of them, and it has become a pass, and there is no order and no notice and nothing with a date on it.** Talla Roke's test — *a route that cannot be used by an ordinary resident is not a public route* — is stated in her own words and entered in the clerk's book on purpose, so that nobody can take it off her by saying she made it up, **and the boundary's answer to it is that it will not fill the form in and forty-one people are not going anywhere.**
- **The public occupancy plan is drafted on the same page the boundary has always kept, is not a new document, is fought over, and is not signed by anybody.** It is extended from ten rows to twelve, the first column takes a second permission the cook chose on purpose, a third row is asked for and refused with two halves of a reason in two mouths, and five people decline to sign for five good reasons. **The first column is a permission and not a count, the fourth column stays empty with its reason in two hands and a modal struck out of one of them, and the plan does not leave the building.**
- **The plan nobody will sign becomes the only plan anybody can use, and it is not a triumph.** **About forty people were moved out of two rooms in fifty minutes out of the third column, in four hands, and the anteroom went above its number and its second way out is a word that nobody in the room can say.** Marek Venn says the sentence and takes half of it back in the same breath, and Renn Vale says she will hold him to the second half for the rest of his life and means it. **A physician then says the page is either the best thing this Hearthhold has done in twenty-seven weeks or the reason one of them is not here in a year, and that he cannot tell you which.**
- **The secondary route is identified at Chapter 140 and it is a place a person can be killed, in five numbered parts, and the fifth part is a refusal this boundary made on the Monday of the same week.** That is better than the prompt asked for: the batch did not merely identify a bad route, it found that the refusal which was correct is also the reason nobody was there.
- **Nothing is resolved and nothing is won.** The room is open and the word is unsaid in it. The fill at the waist is holding with water standing on it and nobody has a number for it. The plan has nobody in it. The review page is blank.
- **The relationship guardrail holds and one of the volume's two remaining acts is spent, and it is the one the prompt named as the stronger.** **Nera Oduya countersigns his revision in her own hand, on a page, with an objection stated first in her own hand above it, in a room with other people in it, and nobody thanks her and nobody asks her why.** The objection is the best argument anybody makes in the batch, it is against him, and it changed what was written. **The remaining act — she stops checking his arithmetic out loud — is deliberately unspent and is recorded as Batch 0005's.** No intimacy, no recognition scene, no acceleration, no scene whose only content is the two of them, and their closest moment is a working moment in a room in which she is right and he has to take it.
- **The working practice holds in every record, and the batch also shows the practice's limit for the first time.** The person who keeps a room writes the change to it: a schoolteacher's count refused to a surface federation, a keeper of terms writing a stronger condition than the man who proposed one, a woman with a rule striking a line out in nine seconds and giving no reason at all. **And a man who finds a lie on a page about a room cannot correct it, because he does not keep the room, and says so in a room before he does anything else — which is the practice working exactly as designed and costing him the only thing he wanted to do with what he found.**
- **The people's guardrails hold.** The woman from the wash rooms is never named, asked, rostered or sent for, **her covers were struck out of a fifth instrument in nine seconds before anybody asked, and the reason was not given at all for the first time in twenty-six weeks**, and the floor is not approached and nobody goes near it. The ninth room is not sent for, the four treads are not walked, **no mark is cut in the stone anywhere in this building**, and the four marks and the groove on the lintel are read two ways and answer nothing. Pell Roke is on the bench, reads numbers aloud, and does not cross the waist. **Talla Roke is in the building, on her own terms, with her own three named people, and is not a rescue, and she is not offered her brother anything and he is not used as a route to her.** Iven Tallo is not redeemed, not a defector, not a martyr and not a saint, and he is not a signatory of anything. Oren Sill is not named. Dael Harrow is not named. Leena Orr's *a list is not a location* is used once, by her, where it earns itself.
- **The two numbers are not joined.** **The eleven that was in the room and the ten in the plan's first column never appear in the same breath in any chapter, and Chapter 135 makes that restraint a decision with a reason in it rather than passing over it in silence.** The clerk enters that nobody in a room with a stranger in it said it, and enters why, and a schoolteacher tells a surface federation that this building has a count and will not give it away and does not say the number.

## Defects found and repaired

1. **A residual duplicated paragraph was found in Chapter 128, outside this batch, and repaired.** The Batch 0003 review record states that all six paragraph-index duplicates were repaired. **One of them was not: the line *Dalla Sen said the thing she had come to say, which was not a fact*, stood twice in `chapters/volume-03/chapter-0128.md`, immediately above her own entry about the fourth column's reason.** It has been removed. **Nothing else in Chapter 128 was touched and no prose of it was changed.** The repair is recorded in `state/continuity.md` under *Volume 03, Batch 0004 — delivered* and in `state/batch-summary.md` rather than in the Batch 0003 record, because the Batch 0003 record is not this phase's file. **This is the second time the class has survived a recorded repair, and it is the class the Batch 0002 independent review found in a Batch 0003 self-review, and the rule is now three batches old: recount from the files after any structural edit, and do not trust an editing session's account of what it removed.**
2. **Three automated markup passes went wrong and all three were repaired.** A script written to normalise quotation marks across the ten files split six multi-paragraph speeches by closing a quote in the middle of one, and quoted five paragraphs of narration. A second pass over-corrected thirty-one section dividers into quoted speech. A third left five narration paragraphs quoted and two paragraphs with an unbalanced quotation count. **All ten files were put into one form — every speech paragraph opens and closes its own quotation, every section divider is a bold paragraph with no quotation — and then verified file by file: every file even on asterisks and on quotation marks, every paragraph with an even quotation count, no nested span, no trailing run of three or more asterisks, and no duplicated paragraph.** **Only quotation marks moved. No prose was reworded by any of the three passes.** The lesson for Batch 0005 is stated plainly: **a batch that repairs prose by paragraph index will duplicate paragraphs, and a batch that repairs markup by script will break prose, and both were found here only by recounting the files rather than by trusting the edit.**
3. **The connective tic was cut rather than managed.** On the first measure the batch carried 274 bold-opened paragraphs, of which 105 opened on *And*, 57 on *The* and 12 on *Nobody*, against Batch 0003's 20, 22 and 2. **Sixty-six openers were rewritten into other shapes** — *A physician was asked*, *At the fourth bell*, *Eighteen mornings in*, *One document in four parts*, *Somebody wanted a third row*, *For a while the room was quiet*, *There was no signature on it*, *Not one person watched them go* — **and the divider count settled at 78 and the including-measure *And* count at 11, against Batch 0003's 20.** The lever is the one the Batch 0001 review named and Batches 0002 and 0003 used: **cut the bold section dividers, not the plain openings.**
4. **The calendar was wrong in three places and was corrected against the week clock.** **The market day was described as not being this week in Chapter 133, as tomorrow in Chapter 139, and as in the morning in Chapter 140. It is the sixth day of the twenty-seventh week and is now that in all three.** Chapter 138's *the day after tomorrow* was wrong from a Wednesday and is now *this week has not got long left in it*.
5. **A locked-road figure was attached to the wrong gate in Chapter 140 and removed.** Chapter 140 read *four households at a second gate four days up the middle passage had nothing*. **The prompt forbids stating a distance for the second gate. The four days belong to the middle gate and the second gate is now *up the middle passage* with no figure on it.**
6. **Three week figures in the twenty-sixth week were a week behind and were corrected.** *This boundary has spent twenty-five weeks learning that*, *has spent twenty-five weeks finding out*, *It has been a word for twenty-five weeks* and *the anteroom above her was the room it had been for twenty-five weeks* all stood in Chapters 131–134, which are the twenty-sixth week. **All four now read twenty-six.** The review page and the plan are at twenty-seven in the twenty-seventh week, so the week-tracking counts are monotonic across the batch. The reconciliation counter was carried unchanged at *twelve weeks* in every mouth and was not re-derived.
7. **One meta leak was cut.** Chapter 140's opening line read *a courier asked the question this volume has been carrying*. **It now reads *a courier asked a question this boundary has been carrying since the first week of the twentieth week*.** The three surviving uses of *in this book* were checked against the canon and are all in Dalla Sen's mouth or in the clerk's own entry, which is the use the canon licenses at Chapters 117 and 118. *The volume* and *the batch* appear nowhere in the prose.
8. **A line was attributed to the wrong mouth and moved.** Chapter 140's first draft gave *a list is not a location* to Marek Venn. **It is Leena Orr's sentence and it now is hers**, where it earns itself: she uses it to stop a man who has laid a length of rope on a floor and called it a way out. Using a character's own sentence in another character's mouth would have been the more damaging kind of error and it was caught by the guardrail check rather than by the measurement.
