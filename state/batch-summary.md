## Movement

- **Chapter 51** opens on a wet tenth week with Renn Vale's morning walk and Jo Verrick's fourteen mornings of measuring the near landing's lip. The method is staged and objected to and the objection improves it: Leena Orr wants the method before the number, Ilsa Norr makes Jo give a **range** instead of a figure because Jo cannot tell how much of the quarter-inch is the stone and how much is the string. The range is **a finger and three-quarters to two inches**. Tams reads the stores and says the sentence that has not changed: a post wants nine foot and a bundle and this building has four foot eleven and three lengths that are a shore's head. Nera takes the first card off the valve board and reads line nine — *a method for a room that one person is going into **once*** — and refuses to hand over a fifth card until the room has decided what the word is for. **Sefu Aran names the work** in his own words, after saying the naming can be struck by whoever he names it to, out loud: *a shore under the near landing's lip, so that the route can be used on a day when the bearing hand is Jo Verrick and Jo Verrick is on the lever.* The receiver is the keeper. Nera ends the chapter stopping Marek in four words, and he shuts his mouth about it, and the shutting is the first honest thing he does all week.
- **Chapter 52** puts the word *once* to the room in Sefu's hand and takes an hour and a half and nine people, four of whom say it wrong on purpose so that the fifth can say it right. The answer is **Renn Vale's**: a card is not a permit for a room, it is a permit for a **work**, and it is **spent when the work is finished or called off**. Nera writes the **fourth document** line by line with four people objecting, keeps the Chapter 42 first line (*the work is a proposal before this boundary*) on it even though the work is now named, and writes **spent** on it and nothing else. **Jo Verrick names the price before anybody asks**: the housing is not opened, therefore the seat is not touched, but the check is open for the whole draw, and the check chatters and packs, and the price of five foot nine **is the check**. Tams puts the hand's date on the board — a month from a day in the seventh week, so the eleventh week — and asks the room not to be kind to him about it. Line seven is the person, and Jo names herself, and says out loud that she has been the only bearing hand in the building since the Thursday, and Renn Vale says the two cannot be done, and Jo says **then the work cannot be done either**, and Sefu carries the sentence.
- **Chapter 53** is the descent, and it is the first chapter in the batch that turns on a person doing a job. Nera releases the eight-foot draw cord for the **fifth** time. Jo goes down, reports the shelf, and — asked by the air engineer what nobody has ever asked her, because nobody has been in that room — reports a **tiled floor under the silt** and **three rows of peg holes in the wall behind the shelf**, in her own words, entered as a witness's words and not a clerk's, at Ilsa Norr's insistence. The check then goes flat and stops chattering, Jo comes up with nothing in a minute and forty, and the card is declared spent — and **Dalla Sen reads the card's own line back** rather than rewriting it, because the draw failed and **the work has not**, and a card is spent when the work ends. Nera writes the number **three** on it in her own hand. The length comes up on the fourth interval, hauled by **Tams Quill and Leena Orr** — arranged over half a day and argued over twice — and is **received by Sefu Aran in the register, item, condition, time, no claim**, and Nera closes her own inventory line and asks for the number to stay on the end of it. **Talla Roke comes down nine steps at half past the fifth bell with a folded paper, and a clerk with her, and a boy on a bench at the top of the nine steps says *that is my sister* without moving, and the clerk writes the fact down with a time on it.**
- **Chapter 54** builds the shore. Iven says *this is not a post* and prices the material shortage out loud, including the reach (**four foot two off the stone**, a different datum from the older shore's four foot seven off the wall, and he insists on the distinction) and the fact that **the head wants a wedge and he has nothing**. Renn has already refused the school-door head its fourth wedge and the refusal stands, so the new shore gets **no wedge at all** and is packed instead, and Tams reads Oren Sill's packing method out of the *carried* column longhand in his left hand because he is frightened of losing it. Renn refuses, in advance and in her own hand, to be on the landing while it is packed. Marek puts his palm on the stone over the lip and gets nothing, and writes **null** for the **seventh** time, and Jo pockets the slate without being asked. Pell asks, in front of the room, that nobody build a plan on him. - **Chapter 55** is the courier. Talla Roke's order is lawful, witnessed, and names a receiver, and it is exactly the paper Iven Tallo asked for in Chapter 12. ## Relationship, thematic, and power change

Marek's Chapter 40 refusal to mark a stone in a mechanism he does not own is answered in this batch by **a route that is worked on by the people who sleep under it** and by a clerk's form that has no line for the only thing that is actually keeping it. Jo Verrick's four words — *you do not have to; you have never once had to* — are the volume's diagnosis of him, and his own correction in Chapter 59 is an admission rather than an insight, which is where Volume 01 left him and no further.

Nera's refusal to publish a rate is no longer an abstraction. In Chapter 57 she claims what it costs — **a day of school, a shut route, a shut route with a reason in a woman's hand** — and calls it a debt that gets paid in somebody's day, and keeps the watch, and does not change it. In Chapter 58 she finds the limit of her own method: she cannot certify a room she has not stood in, there is a room eleven feet down she cannot stand in, and the only honest instrument she has is a bar against a wall and a person writing down *nothing*.

**No power stage is granted.** Marek has **seven nulls** and nothing usable. The route, the check, and the platform are all worked by people who live in the building, and the crew's hours did not go up while the boundary's went from forty-six to fifty-eight.
