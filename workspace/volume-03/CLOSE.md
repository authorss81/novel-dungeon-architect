### Where the prose and `outline/volume-03.md` differ, and why

1. **The climax's placement.** The outline places the holding at 145 and the brought-out at 147; `outline/volume-03.md`'s own batch map also places the holding at 145. **The holding is at 145 and the movement is at 146 and the brought-out is at 147, and the holding runs across the sixth and seventh days of the twenty-eighth week, which are not chapters.** The consequence is that a man is in a channel for two and a half days, and the cost is cumulative and permanent, and the prose says so.
2. **The corridor's open days.** The arrangement shuts the corridor on the first, second and fourth days, and the network had to be *used*, and the movement needed it open. **The resolution is that the woman who keeps the corridor wrote a change to it in her own hand, with a condition and no number and no day on it, and opened it on two normally-shut days. No closure was removed and no school day was restored, because the school does not run on those two days and lost nothing.** This is the working practice applied to the volume's own standing arrangement and it is the outline's own rule doing the work.
3. **The wash-room striking-out in Chapter 142** is on a clerk's working list of rooms, not on a legal instrument, and in Chapter 149 it is on a printed schedule attached to a Compact regime. **The prompt's rule is that her covers stay struck out of every legal instrument including anything the Compact or the guild produce, and that the striking is hers, happens in nine seconds before anybody has asked, and that no reason is to be given now.** Both instances are nine seconds, both before anybody asked, and no reason was given on either.
4. **The refusal of a row for the low end is honoured and not broken.** The outline's batch map left it either way. **It is honoured: the plan has sixteen rows and none of them is the low end of the corridor, both halves of the refusal were restated in one sentence each in a room and were not re-argued, and the cost is that a person went to a place with no plan because nobody was going to be sent to a place with a plan that nobody had been asked to write.** The breaking is the going, not the row.
5. **A sixteenth entry on the board.** The prompt allowed one on its own terms and not as a null, not the claim, not a row and not a plan. **It is a condition, entered by the clerk in Chapter 148, in the column with nothing over the top of it, and the reason the count gave for that column is the volume's argument about a heading.** The board is sixteen entries, nine ordinary and seven in the unheaded column.

## Reconciliation against `outline/ending.md`

**Volume 03 is Volume 03 of sixteen and it is not the last, and this file records what the ending needs and what it has.**

**What Volume 03 delivers that `outline/ending.md` requires later:**

- **Essential routes and records public without being captured.** **The plan is public and it is in a coat and it has no name on it and no signature on it, and it was read out loud in a room to a person from another office with no copy given, and the instrument that made that possible was written by the stranger.** That is the ending's requirement, arrived at from the opposite direction: a city does not need to own a record to be able to show it.
- **A public route that a stranger's mark can be used on.** The market route lost its exclusivity at a counter because a mark cannot be entered in a register, and the answer to the volume's question — *can a route be a public good when the people who use it live on different sides of the law* — is that nobody in this building knows yet, and a competent person at the same counter expects the answer to be reversed in a month. **Volume 04 inherits that expectation.**
- **A closure can save a structure and cost a room, and the two facts do not argue with each other.** A room was shut with eleven people in it on a Friday at the fifth bell, on a competent engineer's recommendation, by the person who keeps it, on a rule everybody followed correctly, and **it is open now under a plan that is the same page, and a physician has put his name against certifying it.** That is the ending's *the people trained to maintain it must say when it is unsafe*, one volume early and in a worse room.
- **Marek's flaw — control disguised as responsibility.** The volume's last movement on it is that he offers to go into a channel because he does not keep a room, and that a woman with a wheel stops the holding without being asked and without a reason he can argue with, and that he says in a room that he is going to be told things about himself by other people for the rest of his life and is not going to be consulted on any of it. **And a clerk asks him in a room what he is going to do when the weather turns, and he does not answer, and she does not ask twice, and it is entered as a question and not as a finding.** That is the flaw named in a room and not cured, and it is the correct place to leave it.
- **A person under a lip, packed and not held, with a post that has never been built.** Untouched in all fifty chapters. Carried forward.

**What Volume 03 does not deliver and must not be recorded as delivering:**

- **It does not deliver Nera Oduya's theory.** She has had half of it since the fourth day of the eleventh week and said so out loud twice, and it is bound to *when this boundary is keeping itself*, and **Volume 03 approached that condition four times and did not meet it**, and nobody asked her and no room may ask. **The theory is Volume 06's.**
- **It does not resolve the stair.** Four treads, dry, unwalked, nobody will walk them, no mark cut in the stone, the ninth line is *none*, and the far wall of the throat is eleven feet down and has never been seen.
- **It does not explain the Lattice's memory.** The first column, the nine-hundred-year-old dowel, the mouth that has been open since the twenty-second week, the four unwalked treads, the ninth room with no row, and the air engineer's theory are **six facts and nobody joined any two of them in a room in fifty chapters**, and a clerk entered that restraint more than once, and the restraint is the answer and not the mystery.
- **It does not fix the charter's hole.** It made the hole one institution deeper and then used the second institution's own procedure, and a federation's route inspector said out loud that the price of that is the price and she would not give it up either. **The hole is worse and it is legible, and that is what Volume 04 needs.**
- **It does not name the One-Seat, does not bring Cassian Rook forward, and introduces no new final enemy.** A Quiet Hands cell is rung three on the antagonist ladder, uncaught, unrecruited, and its dead are dead.
