# Continuity State

## Bootstrap status

No chapter prose has been generated. The following is planned canon established by the bible, series outline, ending outline, and Volume 01 cards.

## Opening canon

- Marek Venn is a 32-year-old civil engineer for Marrowgate Public Works, specializing in shoring, drainage, confined-space rescue, temporary bridges, and evacuation.
- The opening event is a real dry-service-tunnel collapse beneath Marrowgate. An ancient Lattice pressure seam contributes to the failure, while official pressure and ignored warnings prevent Marek from receiving sole blame or complete exoneration. Before the collapse, Marek refuses to sign Public Works Form 7-B, a partial-closure certificate for the unused North Relief Branch that would have allowed the East Spur to reopen under a one-shift clearance.
- Marek enters the Lattice carrying his missing father Alden Venn's damaged brass survey rule. The rule is an imperfect reader, not a chosen-one artifact.
- The opening party is exactly six: Marek, Leena Orr, Iven Tallo, Pell Roke, Oren Sill, and Tams Quill. The shift begins with twenty-four liters of water and ordinary rescue gear; after the collapse, the recoverable baseline is eight liters, a frayed nine-meter line, one cracked hand lamp, a folding rule, grease pencil, damaged chalk, a surviving screw jack, one pry bar, two short timber uprights, a damaged but recoverable hand-tool kit, and the brass rule. The ration ledger is eight liters recovered, six after Chapter 2, five after Chapter 3, and one additional liter of verified condensation reserve in Chapter 6; the Chapter 9 pump draws from the flooded sump, not the drinking supply.
- Leena dislocates her right shoulder while pulling Pell clear in Chapter 1. Marek's right hand is crushed between a jack and stone during the controlled Chapter 5 breach, leaving permanent fine-control loss; he adapts with his left hand and assistance. Pell twists his left ankle when a returning-stair step settles in Chapter 8. Oren remains alive through the first batch, is established as a second shoring and stretcher hand, and is reserved for a later Hearthhold-crossing death caused by route capacity.
- Nera Oduya is an independent ventilation engineer from Hearthhold Middle. At her first meeting she rejects the recorded air plan of Marek's failed refuge, not a new generic route draft. Sefu Aran, an elected Hearthhold boundary route keeper, has a separate civic veto over admission; neither speaks for every Hearthhold.
- Dael Harrow is a Cartwright clearance master and the first institutional antagonist. The Ninefold Charter, Stonelight Compact, Seatwardens, and Quiet Hands are established factions, not last-minute additions.
## World rules

- The Lattice is a self-repairing ancient refuge built by the Cairn Commons. It responds to load, flow, repeated use, and imperfect material memory; it is not a single conscious god.
- The five Room Checks are bearing, breath, drainage, escape, and change. A stable structure can still be unsafe for the people inside it.
- Loadcraft can read relative force, place a limited bearing mark, build a counter-braced structure, change a route, bind a structure to repeated use, or coordinate a relay. It cannot create matter, grant air, solve politics, predict the future, or automatically repair a room.
- Every useful route change has a counter-load, a material cost, a labor cost, and a political or social consequence.
- Faultforms are physical hazards generated by unresolved room functions. They can be evaded, repurposed, or resolved by changing the function or load; violence alone is not a complete answer. The first returning stair uses a visible movable landing, counterweight, and stone throat: its old upper walking route and lower stretcher route became unbalanced when the lower receiver collapsed, so human weight physically cycles the landing instead of teleporting anyone.
- A coercive or privately controlled public structure is unstable in both the physical and civic sense. Consent is a load-system rule. At a Hearthhold boundary, Nera's air authority and Sefu's civic admission veto are separate; a passing air test is not consent.

## History and mystery

- The Cairn Commons built the Lattice as a refuge against the Long Undertow.
- The Cairn Directorate added the One-Seat as an emergency centralized control during the catastrophe. It caused the Sundering.
- The original Civic Loom distributed load and maintenance among independent anchors.
- The Long Undertow's original cause and the contents of the Unmapped Black remain intentionally open.
- Alden died in the Sable Cut while sheltering stranded workers. His final design was communal and maintainable. He is not resurrected or turned into a secret power source.

## Progression

The planned power order is: unschooled eye → Reader → Marker → Bracer → Router → District engineer → Loomwright → Commons architect / anchorless steward. Stage boundaries are approximate and must be earned through practice, failure, relationships, labor, and public responsibility.

Marek's long-term physical cost is load echoes, vertigo, unstable balance, permanent fine-control loss in one hand, and eventual permanent sensory damage caused by the final unshared intervention. The final shock is cumulative; it must not contradict the opening state.

## First-batch mechanical and legal continuity

- Water rises through an ordinary wet mineral pressure seam, not a rule-line. The failed reciprocating sump pump has a shattered bronze piston gland and a missing drive-dog key; the hinged flood gate is only a temporary isolation measure. Chapter 9's Hearthhold service canister supplies a split gland seal and key. The repair must be installed and tested: twenty no-load strokes, five minutes of draw-down, a fifteen-centimeter drop, a dry gland, unchanged air cloth, and a dry flood gate. The delivery check still chatters, so the pump is attended and temporary.
- A Cartwright clearance seal is tied to a route's survey date, occupancy, and conditions. Dael's courier reaches the group through the licensed inspection incline established in Chapter 3, carrying a temporary route-custody order, an old seal, a salvage lien, and an insurance-forfeiture notice. The order can lock the guild door and freeze guild-funded equipment; it cannot open the Hearthhold gate, order air, or establish ownership.
- Chapter 10's anteroom test is twelve adults (the six crew, Nera, Sefu, and four Hearthhold workers), one stretcher, and 120 kilograms of gear for thirty minutes with the pump running. Its limited working rating is ten people including one stretcher for two hours, no open flame, outer service door open as the escape route, and Sefu or Nera empowered to stop it. Airflow reversal or water over the sill sends everyone to the upper service landing. The test is a conditional pass, not a permanent route approval.
- Marek's Chapter 10 non-claim receipt relinquishes only priority to the dismantled Hearthhold case and unrecovered Cinder Steps material under Dael's notice. It does not surrender public-works tools, the brass rule, the route, or Hearthhold consent.

## Ending guardrail

The final resolution converts the One-Seat into a public, distributed relay and creates the self-governing city of Morrowlight. Marek does not become king or sole administrator. Cassian Rook survives under public limits. Nera, Iven, Talla, Mero, Iona, and the Hearthholds retain independent agency.
