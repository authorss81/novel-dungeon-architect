## Chapter 0101 — Under The School Floor

- **Location/time:** The school corner at the school-door end of the corridor, and the anteroom. **Twentieth week, first day, a Monday. The corridor is shut and the school is not running.**

The anteroom can only be emptied in the pump's window, and the pump's window is the afternoon. **The room with the wet floor is six feet from the wall with the seam in it**, and the seam is the boundary's drinking water, and the two jars a day are the only drinking water there is. The seam's threads have not increased; **the seam has simply found the low side of a floor nobody has looked at.** The school corner's mouth is shut, warm, and going into the throat, and it has been shut for twenty weeks, and no rate has been published. The floor is dished toward the school door, and it is dished because of wear, and wear is a use.
