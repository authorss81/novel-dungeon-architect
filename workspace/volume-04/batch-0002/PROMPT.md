**The hand-over, in the form Volume 04 Batch 0003 will need it:** a brace standing and watched, with the name of the person who may stop it in a clerk's book and not on a page; **a school door with no wedge in it and the question of what watches a door that is still open;** a wall with a chalk line and a string on it and a fortnight of figures that a clerk reads and a physician does not; **a pot at nine days and a seam going quiet and four households at a second gate who have nothing;** a man with a post in a carrier's hand that he has neither refused nor accepted; **a carpenter whose hand does not close and who is holding joinwood;** a store four days up the middle passage whose fir is going up the road on the Monday and whose beech stays and whose list has no date on it; **a schedule with a column headed for the person who will be present and a third room still standing on it;** a board at seventeen or eighteen entries with eight or nine under a column with nothing over the top of it; **a question in four words on the page in a clerk's book in a man's own hand, asked and not answered;** and a man whose left hand has not closed since the twenty-eighth week and who has a standing now, because an air engineer asked him for a work in a room and said out loud that a standing is the first half of a post.
